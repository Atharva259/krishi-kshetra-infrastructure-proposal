## 🚀 The Local Sovereign Cloud

Instead of paying massive OpEx to AWS or Azure, Krishi Kshetra leverages its **₹120.00 Crore** budget to build an exascale-capable local AI infrastructure.

## 🖥️ Local Compute Array (₹1.50 Cr)
* **Lean Local Server Nodes:** Rack-mounted AMD EPYC / Intel Xeon Scalable nodes with 512GB ECC DDR5 RAM.
* **The Single-Tank Compute Pod:** Housed in a self-contained single-phase immersion cooling tank with an integrated mini Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU), ensuring perfectly silent, thermally unlimited model training on-site.

## 💻 Edge AI Workstations (₹10.00 Cr)
Training and inference occur primarily at the desks of our elite engineers:
* **Founder & Core AI Rigs:** Intel Core Ultra 9 285K matched with NVIDIA RTX 5090 (32GB GDDR7) GPUs, 256GB RAM, cooled by massive 420mm AIOs on 1500W Platinum PSUs.
* **Mobile AI Fleet:** Lenovo Legion Pro 9i laptops running Core Ultra 9 and RTX 5090 mobile variants, sustained by 250W high-capacity Anker Prime power banks for field research.

## 🗄️ High-Speed Training Storage
* Training datasets bypass internet bottlenecks by pulling directly from the local Multi-Petabyte Corporate NAS via 100GbE fiber lines, caching active datasets onto local Gen 5 NVMe tiers.

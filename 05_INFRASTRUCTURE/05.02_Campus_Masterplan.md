## 🏗️ Master Architectural Layout

The Krishi Kshetra sovereign campus is engineered for extreme resilience, thermal efficiency, and physical security under the **₹120.00 Crore** infrastructure allocation.

## ⚡ Sustainable Microgrid & Power (₹12.00 Cr)
The campus is completely insulated from grid failures:
* **2.5 MW Solar PV Farm:** Ground and roof-mounted arrays providing baseline load.
* **4 MWh BESS Container:** Heavy battery storage for continuous operations during low-generation periods.
* **Green Hydrogen Backup:** Zero-emission continuous backup generators.
* **Dual 33kV Substations:** K-rated step-down stations handling high-density immersion compute loads.

## ❄️ Thermal Infrastructure (₹15.00 Cr)
* **Industrial Immersion Cooling Network:** Single-phase dielectric immersion tanks spread across the compute zones.
* **Fluid Logistics:** Central dry cooling units and smart distribution manifolds mounted externally to reject heat silently.
* **Safety Buffers (₹2.50 Cr):** Reserve dielectric fluid stocks, optical leak-detection arrays, and Novec/FM-200 clean-agent fire suppression.

## 🌐 Enterprise Storage & Network (₹6.00 Cr)
* Dual-ISP BGP network hardware (Tata + Airtel/Jio) for seamless failover.
* Multi-petabyte corporate NAS arrays utilizing PCIe Gen 5 NVMe fast-caching tiers to completely bypass external data centers.

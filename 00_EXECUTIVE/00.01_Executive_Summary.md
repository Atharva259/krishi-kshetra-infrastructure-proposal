## 1. Administrative & Strategic Overview

The modernization of the agricultural sector is no longer merely a matter of yield optimization; it is a critical pillar of national security, economic resilience, and ecological sustainability. Krishi Kshetra represents a pragmatic, capital-backed approach to building a long-term, custom-owned technological foundation for agriculture in India.

By integrating high-reliability Internet of Things (IoT) telemetry, advanced computer vision, autonomous agricultural robotics, on-premise AI supercomputing, and a robust cloud-based software operating system, Krishi Kshetra bridges the gap between laboratory research and actual field implementation.

This document outlines the operational, technological, infrastructure, and financial blueprint of Krishi Kshetra for evaluation by institutional venture capital funds and central incubation bodies (such as NITI Aayog, DST, ICAR-NAARM, and BIRAC). The objective is to secure **₹32 Crores (₹32,00,00,000)** in institutional seed capital to **purchase land and construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus facility over a 1-year period (Year 1)**, deploy **₹4.00 Crores in Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs), **₹3.00 Crores in High-Performance Gaming Laptops**, **₹2.00 Crores in Enterprise AI Foundation Models (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi AI, Adobe Creative Cloud)**, and **₹1.00 Crore in Dedicated High-Speed Dual 10 Gbps Fiber/Satellite Internet**, launching commercial field operations in Year 2.

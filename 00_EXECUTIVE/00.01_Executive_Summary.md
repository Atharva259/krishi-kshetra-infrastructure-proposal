## 1. Administrative & Strategic Overview

The modernization of the agricultural sector is no longer merely a matter of yield optimization; it is a critical pillar of national security, economic resilience, and ecological sustainability. Krishi Kshetra represents a pragmatic, capital-backed approach to building a long-term, custom-owned technological foundation for agriculture in India.

By integrating high-reliability Internet of Things (IoT) telemetry, advanced computer vision, autonomous agricultural robotics, on-premise AI supercomputing, and a robust cloud-based software operating system, Krishi Kshetra bridges the gap between laboratory research and actual field implementation.

This document outlines the operational, technological, infrastructure, and financial blueprint of Krishi Kshetra for evaluation by institutional venture capital funds and central incubation bodies (such as NITI Aayog, DST, ICAR-NAARM, and BIRAC). The objective is to secure **₹32 Crores (₹32,00,00,000)** in institutional seed capital to **purchase land and construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus facility over a 1-year period (Year 1)**, deploy **₹4.00 Crores in high-end Desktop Workstation builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, AIO liquid cooling) and **₹3.00 Crores in Flagship Engineering Laptops**, launching commercial field operations in Year 2.

## 2. Company Overview

### 2.1 Corporate Structure & Profile
Krishi Kshetra is structured as a high-technology enterprise combining industrial prototyping facilities with a cloud-native software engineering division. Operating at the intersection of Mechatronics, Artificial Intelligence, and Agronomy, the company is organized into five specialized divisions:

```
[ Board of Directors / Advisory Panel ]
                 │
      [ Executive Management ]
                 │
 ┌───────────────┼───────────────┬───────────────┐
 │               │               │               │
[R&D Labs] [Software Dev] [Testing & QA] [Field Ops] [Legal & Finance]
```

* **Research & Development Laboratories**: Focuses on mechanical CNC design, industrial SMT assembly, and sensor integration.
* **Software Engineering**: Handles cloud microservices, Kotlin mobile clients, machine learning models, and API integrations.
* **Testing & Quality Assurance**: Operates automated device farms (low-end Android Go/iOS) and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) test benches.
* **Field Operations & Regional Hubs**: Oversees FPO onboarding, equipment deployment, and regional hub operations across Indian agricultural zones.
* **Legal & Finance**: Manages land registration, civil construction permits, global IP patent filings, regulatory compliance (WPC/BIS/CE), and financial governance.

## 5. Long-Term Strategic Vision & Phased 5-Year Roadmap

Under this building construction model, **Year 1 generates ₹0 revenue** while the campus is constructed, with commercial operations commencing in **Year 2**:

* **Year 1 (Building Construction Phase)**: **₹0 Revenue** (100% focused on land acquisition, civil construction, solar grid setup, software/firmware R&D; Net Loss: ₹12.00 Crores OPEX).
* **Year 2 (Facility Launch & Commercial Pilot)**: **₹2.50 Crores Revenue** (First 10,000 acres PAaaS + 50 cold storage retrofits; Net Loss: ₹5.50 Crores).
* **Year 3 (Commercial Expansion)**: **₹18.00 Crores Revenue** (75,000 acres PAaaS + Agri-OS Cloud SaaS; **EBITDA Positive**; Net Profit: **₹2.20 Crores**; Series A round of **₹60 Crores**).
* **Year 4 (Regional Scale)**: **₹65.00 Crores Revenue** (300,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit: **₹14.50 Crores**).
* **Year 5 (National Expansion)**: **₹180.00 Crores Revenue** (900,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit: **₹46.00 Crores**).

## 🌾 Company Overview

Krishi Kshetra is a vertically-integrated agricultural deep-tech company. We do not just build software interfaces; we engineer the physical machines, the silicon architecture, and the neural networks that will automate the future of Indian agriculture.

Backed by a **₹120.00 Crore** core infrastructure foundation, we operate entirely autonomously from external data centers. 

## ⚙️ Core Business Units

### 1. Sovereign AI Development (The Core Brain)
Housed within an Anechoic EMI/EMC chamber and cooled by an industrial single-phase dielectric immersion tank, our local servers process immense agricultural datasets locally, ensuring absolute IP security and zero latency.

### 2. Autonomous Robotics Manufacturing
Our internal 5-axis CNC machines, fiber laser cutters, and SMT pick-and-place lines allow us to prototype, print, and assemble ruggedized agricultural robots (rovers, drones, automated harvesters) entirely on our campus.

### 3. Edge IoT & Sensor Networks
We deploy advanced telemetry arrays to farms, transmitting raw multi-spectral data back to our centralized deep learning clusters via dual-ISP BGP network backbones.

### 4. Enterprise Software Services
The customer-facing digital layer provides farmers and agricultural enterprises with predictive crop planning, algorithmic pricing models, and fleet management dashboards.

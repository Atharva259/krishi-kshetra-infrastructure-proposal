## 🔭 The 2031 Horizon

By 2031, Krishi Kshetra will transition from a highly capitalized R&D facility into India's premier manufacturer of autonomous agricultural hardware and predictive AI systems. The **₹120.00 Crore** initial foundation serves as the launching pad for this dominance.

## 📍 Year-by-Year Milestones

### Year 1: Infrastructure & Immersion (2026-2027)
* Construct the 2.5 MW Solar Microgrid and 4 MWh BESS.
* Deploy the Industrial Immersion Cooling Network and single-tank compute array.
* Bring the Anechoic EMI/EMC chamber online.
* Scale the core engineering team across the 15 custom liquid-cooled desktops.

### Year 2: Deep Learning & Prototyping (2027-2028)
* Launch V1 of the AI Crop Advisor trained entirely on local immersion servers.
* Produce the first 50 physical autonomous harvester prototypes using in-house 5-axis CNC and SMT lines.
* Secure multi-zone Wi-Fi 7 across the Auxiliary Guest Annex.

### Year 3: Edge Deployment (2028-2029)
* Deploy 500 autonomous units to partner farms across India.
* Establish a massive local data-lake piped through our dual-ISP BGP edge routers.
* The 3-Year OPEX runway concludes, shifting the company into heavy revenue generation.

### Year 4: Commercial Scaling (2029-2030)
* Expand the microgrid beyond 5 MW to support expanding factory lines.
* Shift from prototype manufacturing to mass assembly of robotics.
* Launch quantum-resistant hardware security modules (HSMs) on all edge devices.

### Year 5: Market Dominance (2030-2031)
* Achieve absolute dominance in the Indian predictive agriculture space.
* Prepare for global export of sovereign robotic platforms.

## 🔭 The 2036 Horizon: The Agricultural Sovereign

By 2036, Krishi Kshetra will no longer be just a technology company; it will be the foundational operating system for Indian Agriculture, entirely self-sustained on proprietary physical infrastructure. The **₹120.00 Crore** seed allocation built the roots for a multi-billion dollar industrial empire.

## 🚀 Decade Objectives

### 1. Total Grid Independence
The initial 2.5 MW solar microgrid will have scaled into a massive decentralized energy network powering entirely automated smart-factories across multiple states.

### 2. Autonomous Supply Chains
Our robotic fleets will not just harvest crops—they will manage the entire lifecycle from seed planting to predictive yield routing to distribution centers, requiring zero human intervention.

### 3. Exascale Local Compute
The single-tank immersion cooling architecture deployed in 2026 will evolve into subterranean, green-hydrogen-powered exascale data centers, processing global agricultural climate variables entirely on-premise, shielded by Quantum-Resistant HSMs.

### 4. Global Hardware Export
Krishi Kshetra's ruggedized edge devices, originally prototyped in our foundational R&D lab, will be exported globally as the gold standard for secure, autonomous farming in harsh environments.

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra employs a **hybrid hardware-enabled SaaS revenue model**. By pairing low-cost, high-reliability proprietary hardware (Bhumika-1 telemetry nodes and KR-Alpha rovers manufactured on our in-house SMT line) with high-margin recurring software subscriptions (Agri-OS and PAaaS), the company secures long-term predictable recurring revenue while locking in customer retention across Indian agricultural hubs.

Key Targets:
* **Blended Gross Margin**: **66%** by Year 3 (85% on SaaS/APIs, 42% on Hardware).
* **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: **> 120%** via land-and-expand sensor additions.
* **LTV/CAC Ratio**: Target **> 4.5x** with a payback period under **9 months**.

## 3. Stream 1: Precision Agriculture as a Service (PAaaS)

PAaaS lowers adoption barriers for farming cooperatives and FPOs by bundling hardware installation, battery replacement, telemetry monitoring, and diagnostic alerts into a recurring per-acre fee in Indian Rupees.

| Tier | Target Client | Included Hardware | Annual Subscription Fee (₹) | Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PAaaS Starter** | Smallholder Cooperatives / FPOs (<100 acres) | 1 Bhumika-1 node per 10 acres + 1 Vayu-1 Weather Station | ₹1,200 / acre / year | Soil NPK/moisture alerts, 3-day weather risk forecasts, mobile app access. |
| **PAaaS Professional**| Mid-size Agribusinesses (100–1,000 acres) | 1 Bhumika-1 node per 5 acres + Vayu-1 + KR-Alpha Rover service | ₹2,000 / acre / year | All Starter features + automated spot weeding, disease CV scans, and priority support. |
| **PAaaS Enterprise** | Commercial Plantations (>1,000 acres) | High-density node mesh + dedicated on-site rover fleet | Custom (Avg ₹1,600/acre) | Full Agri-OS API access, dedicated agronomist account manager, custom ML models. |

## 10. Glossary
* **ARR**: Annual Recurring Revenue; predictable revenue generated by a business in a year.
* **LTV**: Lifetime Value; total revenue expected from a single customer account.
* **CAC**: Customer Acquisition Cost; total cost of acquiring a new customer.
* **NRR**: Net Revenue Retention; percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers.
* **PAaaS**: Precision Agriculture as a Service; subscription model bundling ag-tech hardware and software.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 1-year land acquisition, civil building construction, **Temporary First Office Renovation & Electrical Rewiring**, **500kVA HT Transformer Substation Application**, **₹4.00 Crores Custom AIO Desktop Builds**, **₹3.00 Crores Gaming Laptops**, and **₹2.00 Crores Unity 3D & Enterprise AI Suite** deployment strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Permanent land purchase, civil campus construction, **First Office Renovation, Furniture Buying & Electrical Rewiring**, **500kVA HT Transformer Substation**, **₹4.00 Crores Custom AIO Builds**, **₹3.00 Crores Gaming Laptops**, SMT line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, and component inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries, **₹2.00 Crores Unity 3D, Autodesk & Enterprise AI Subscriptions**, **₹1.00 Crore High-Speed Dual 10 Gbps Fiber/Satellite Internet**, temporary suite rent, land registration legal fees, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, renovate/rewire our temporary first office suite, apply for a dedicated 500kVA HT transformer substation, equip our teams with **₹4.00 Crores in Custom AIO Desktop Builds** and **₹3.00 Crores in Gaming Laptops**, and establish a **₹2.00 Crore Unity 3D & Enterprise AI Suite**. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

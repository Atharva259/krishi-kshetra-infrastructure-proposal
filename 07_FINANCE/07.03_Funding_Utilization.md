## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 1-year land acquisition and civil building construction strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Land plot purchase, 15,000 sq. ft. civil campus construction, SMT assembly line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, NVIDIA AI compute cluster, and component inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries (software/firmware R&D & civil oversight), temporary office suite rent, land registration legal fees, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot and construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, with commercial field operations commencing in Year 2. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

Fund release is strictly tied to a 3-Tranche Milestone System, ensuring that secondary and tertiary capital releases depend on verified civil construction and technical achievements.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 5. Milestone Verification & Gate Criteria

### 5.1 Gate 1 (Tranche 2 Release Criteria - Month 6)
* **MS-1.1**: Land plot title registration completed and two-story civil building shell structure erected.
* **MS-1.2**: Municipal environmental, safety, and high-voltage power substation permits approved.
* **MS-1.3**: Baseline Agri-OS cloud microservices and Kotlin mobile app running in software simulation.

### 5.2 Gate 2 (Tranche 3 Release Criteria - Month 12)
* **MS-2.1**: Campus construction completed; 250kW solar microgrid operational with 500kWh battery storage.
* **MS-2.2**: SMT pick-and-place line, Haas 5-axis CNC machines, and NVIDIA AI compute node installed and commissioned.
* **MS-2.3**: Year 2 commercial pilot agreements signed with regional FPOs for 10,000 acres.

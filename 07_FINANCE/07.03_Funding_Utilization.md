## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 1-year land acquisition, civil building construction, **₹4.00 Crores Desktop Builds**, and **₹3.00 Crores Laptops** deployment strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Land plot purchase, 15,000 sq. ft. civil campus construction, **₹4.00 Crores in Desktop Builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs, AIO liquid cooling), **₹3.00 Crores in Flagship Laptops**, SMT assembly line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, and component inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries, temporary office suite rent, land registration legal fees, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, and equip our teams with **₹4.00 Crores in Desktop Builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs, AIO liquid cooling) and **₹3.00 Crores in Flagship Laptops**. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

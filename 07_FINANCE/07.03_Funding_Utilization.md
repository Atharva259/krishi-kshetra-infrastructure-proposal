## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹1.60 Crore (₹160.0 Lakhs)** seed funding round. It ensures capital efficiency, audit-ready financial reporting under Indian accounting standards, and alignment between fund release schedules and key engineering/commercial deliverables.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹100.0 Lakhs / 62.5%): Laboratory machinery, solar microgrid, IT/QA testing rigs, and component inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹60.0 Lakhs / 37.5%): Personnel stipends, facility rent, utilities, cloud services, and legal/compliance retainers.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for investors and government incubators (e.g. DST, BIRAC, ICAR-NAARM).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹1.60 Crore (₹160.0 Lakhs)** in seed funding to transition from concept to validated field deployment over a 12-month period in India. To protect investor equity and ensure maximum capital efficiency, funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹100.0 Lakhs (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹60.0 Lakhs (37.5%)**

Fund release is strictly tied to a 3-Tranche Milestone System, ensuring that secondary and tertiary capital releases depend on verified technical and infrastructure achievements.

## 5. Capital Governance & Approval Matrices

All expenditures must strictly follow delegation-of-authority limits in Indian Rupees:

| Single Expense Amount (₹) | Required Approval Level | Verification Document Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **< ₹25,000** | Lead Department Engineer | Standard invoice / GST receipt. |
| **₹25,001 – ₹1,50,000** | VP of Operations / Founder | 2 competitive vendor quotes + PO. |
| **₹1,50,001 – ₹7,50,000** | CEO & CFO Dual Approval | 3 competitive vendor quotes + technical evaluation form. |
| **> ₹7,50,000** | Board of Directors Approval | Formal RFP process + Board resolution. |

## 6. Milestone Verification & Gate Criteria

The release of Tranche 2 and Tranche 3 depends on meeting objective technical criteria signed off by independent auditors or investor representatives:

### 6.1 Gate 1 (Tranche 2 Release Criteria - Month 4)
* **MS-1.1**: Solar microgrid generates at least 15kW peak power with 30kWh battery backup operational.
* **MS-1.2**: Workshop lease finalized and ESD safety grounding installed.
* **MS-1.3**: Baseline Agri-OS cloud microservices running on local build server and AWS.

### 6.2 Gate 2 (Tranche 3 Release Criteria - Month 8)
* **MS-2.1**: 5 Bhumika-1 sensor nodes demonstrate 30-day continuous operation over LoRaWAN with <100μA standby current.
* **MS-2.2**: KR-Alpha rover prototype executes path-following autonomous steering within ±10cm accuracy.
* **MS-2.3**: HIL firmware test rig running automated continuous integration test suites.

## 7. Variance Controls & Contingency Policy

* **Reallocation Threshold**: Department heads may adjust line-item spending within their cost center by up to **±5%** without board approval, provided the total department budget is unchanged.
* **Contingency Fund**: The ₹4.40 Lakhs operational buffer (OP-BUF) is reserved for unforeseen price spikes in raw materials or components.
* **Burn Rate Cap**: If monthly burn exceeds **₹5,50,000/month** (10% above target), a mandatory spending freeze on non-essential subscriptions is triggered automatically.

## 9. Glossary
* **Tranche**: A portion of a key financial release structured under specific conditions.
* **WBS**: Work Breakdown Structure; a deliverable-oriented breakdown of a project into smaller components.
* **RFP**: Request for Proposal; a document that solicits proposals by a company interested in procurement.
* **CA**: Chartered Accountant; an accredited financial auditor under Indian statutory guidelines.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 1-year land acquisition, civil building construction, **₹4.00 Crores Custom AIO Desktop Builds**, **₹3.00 Crores Gaming Laptops**, **₹2.00 Crores Enterprise AI Suite (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi AI, Adobe)**, and **₹1.00 Crore High-Speed Internet** deployment strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Land plot purchase, 15,000 sq. ft. civil campus construction, **₹4.00 Crores in Custom AIO Desktop Builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs), **₹3.00 Crores in Gaming Laptops**, SMT assembly line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, and component inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries, **₹2.00 Crores in Enterprise AI Subscriptions (Claude, ChatGPT, Kimi AI, Adobe)**, **₹1.00 Crore in High-Speed Dual 10 Gbps Fiber/Satellite Internet**, temporary office suite rent, land registration legal fees, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, equip our teams with **₹4.00 Crores in Custom AIO Desktop Builds** and **₹3.00 Crores in Gaming Laptops**, and establish a **₹2.00 Crore Enterprise AI Suite**. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

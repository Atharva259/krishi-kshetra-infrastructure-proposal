### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round. * **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Manufacturing machinery, SMT assembly line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, NVIDIA AI compute cluster, and silicon inventories.

It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to construct an industrial tech campus, manufacture 10,000 soil nodes and 100 autonomous rovers, and deploy across 50,000 acres over an 18-month period. To protect investor equity and guarantee execution, funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

Fund release is strictly tied to a 3-Tranche Milestone System, ensuring that secondary and tertiary capital releases depend on verified technical and infrastructure achievements.

## 5. Capital Governance & Approval Matrices

All expenditures must strictly follow delegation-of-authority limits in Indian Rupees:

| Single Expense Amount (₹) | Required Approval Level | Verification Document Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **< ₹1,00,000** | Lead Department Engineer | Standard invoice / GST receipt. |
| **₹1,00,001 – ₹10,00,000** | VP of Operations / Founder | 2 competitive vendor quotes + PO. |
| **₹10,00,001 – ₹50,00,000** | CEO & CFO Dual Approval | 3 competitive vendor quotes + technical evaluation form. |
| **> ₹50,00,000** | Board of Directors Approval | Formal RFP process + Board resolution. |

## 6. Milestone Verification & Gate Criteria

### 6.1 Gate 1 (Tranche 2 Release Criteria - Month 5)
* **MS-1.1**: 250kW solar microgrid generates at least 200kW peak power with 500kWh battery backup operational.
* **MS-1.2**: Tech campus lease finalized, 15,000 sq. ft. floor space prepared, and ESD grounding installed.
* **MS-1.3**: On-premise NVIDIA HGX AI compute cluster operational and running local baseline vision model training.

### 6.2 Gate 2 (Tranche 3 Release Criteria - Month 11)
* **MS-2.1**: 10,000 Bhumika-1 nodes pass SMT automated optical inspection (AOI) with >99% pass rate.
* **MS-2.2**: 100 KR-Alpha rovers execute path-following autonomous steering within ±5cm accuracy.
* **MS-2.3**: HIL firmware test rig running 100% automated continuous integration suites.

## 7. Variance Controls & Contingency Policy

* **Reallocation Threshold**: Department heads may adjust spending within their cost center by up to **±5%** without board approval, provided total budget is unchanged.
* **Contingency Reserve**: The ₹1.00 Crore operational buffer is reserved for unforeseen component price swings.
* **Burn Rate Cap**: If monthly burn exceeds **₹1.10 Crores/month** (10% above target), a mandatory recruitment freeze is triggered automatically.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 1-year land acquisition, civil building construction, **Temporary First Office Renovation & Electrical Rewiring**, **500kVA HT Transformer Substation Application**, **Mahindra XUV700 Ebony Dark Edition SUV Fleet (4 @ ₹35L each)**, **Concert-Grade PA Sound Systems**, **GaN Fast Chargers & Vehicle 230V Inverters**, **Multi-Operator 5G SIM Cellular Failover**, **Unlisted Soundproof Production Studio**, **Cinema Camera Fleet**, **Lab Testing Equipment**, **Outdoor Advertising**, **₹3.00 Crores Custom AIO Desktop Builds**, **₹2.00 Crores Gaming Laptops**, and **₹2.00 Crores Unity 3D & Enterprise AI Suite** deployment strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): Permanent land purchase, civil campus construction, First Office Fit-Out, 500kVA HT Substation, **Mahindra XUV700 Ebony Dark Edition Fleet (4 @ ₹35L)**, **Concert PA Sound Systems**, **GaN Fast Chargers**, 4K Laser Projectors & 86" Displays, Unlisted Studio & Cinema Cameras, Lab Equipment, Custom AIO Builds, Gaming Laptops, SMT line, 250kW solar microgrid.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries, **₹2.00 Crores Unity 3D, Autodesk & Enterprise AI Subscriptions**, **₹1.50 Crores High-Speed Dual 10 Gbps Fiber, Starlink & Multi-Operator 5G Enterprise SIMs**, **₹0.50 Crores XUV700 Fleet Diesel Fuel & Servicing**, **₹1.00 Crore Outdoor Advertising & Field Filming**, temporary suite rent, land registration legal fees, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, renovate/rewire our temporary first office suite, apply for a 500kVA HT transformer substation, procure Mahindra XUV700 Ebony Dark Edition SUVs (@ ₹35L each), deploy concert PA sound systems and GaN fast chargers, build an **unlisted soundproof production studio & cinema camera fleet**, establish **multi-operator 5G SIM cellular failover**, and launch **outdoor advertising & field trip filming**. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹50.00 Crores (₹5,000 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to purchase a land plot, construct a custom 25,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus over a **1-year construction period (Year 1)**, establish a **1,500 sq. ft. Executive Investor Boardroom with 110" MicroLED 4K Video Wall**, deploy a **structural metal chassis 5-axis CNC machining center**, **industrial 3D printed body production line**, **Yamaha SMT PCB line**, procure an **electronics & agronomy lab testing equipment suite**, build a **media production studio & Sony FX6/FX3 cinema fleet**, deploy **DJI Inspire 3 8K enterprise recording drones**, renovate/rewire our temporary first office suite, apply for a 500kVA HT transformer substation, deploy a **500 kVA campus silent generator**, procure **portable silent inverter generators**, **portable battery wireless printers**, **high-speed duplex document & ID card scanners**, **foldable field desks**, procure Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs (@ ₹35L each), deploy concert PA sound systems and GaN fast chargers, establish **multi-operator 5G SIM cellular failover**, and launch **outdoor advertising, media content production & field trip filming**. Funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹30.00 Crores (60.0%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (40.0%)**

### Purpose
This document specifies the strategy, milestone gates, governance policies, and tracking framework for utilizing the **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** institutional seed funding round under a 90-day rapid tech campus buildout strategy.

### Scope
This specification governs the deployment of all seed round capital across:
* **CAPEX Investments** (₹20.00 Crores / 62.5%): 90-day workspace construction, SMT assembly line, 5-axis CNC mill, 250kW solar microgrid, NVIDIA AI compute cluster, and silicon inventories.
* **OPEX & Working Capital** (₹12.00 Crores / 37.5%): 25+ senior engineering salaries, campus & regional hub leases, cloud compute, patent filings, and reserve buffer.
It defines tranches, release triggers, variance thresholds, and reporting schedules for institutional investors and central government bodies (NITI Aayog, DST, BIRAC).

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra is raising **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** in institutional seed funding to construct a 15,000 sq. ft. industrial tech campus in **under 90 days**, manufacture 10,000 soil nodes and 100 autonomous rovers, and deploy across a conservative 5,000-acre initial pilot. To protect investor equity and guarantee execution, funds are allocated across two core pools:

* **Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)**: **₹20.00 Crores (62.5%)**
* **Operating Expenditure (OPEX)**: **₹12.00 Crores (37.5%)**

Fund release is strictly tied to a 3-Tranche Milestone System, ensuring that secondary and tertiary capital releases depend on verified technical and infrastructure achievements.

## 5. Milestone Verification & Gate Criteria

### 5.1 Gate 1 (Tranche 2 Release Criteria - Month 5)
* **MS-1.1**: 90-day tech campus buildout completed; 250kW solar microgrid operational with 500kWh battery storage.
* **MS-1.2**: 15,000 sq. ft. campus floor space prepared, anti-static ESD grounding installed.
* **MS-1.3**: On-premise NVIDIA HGX AI compute cluster operational and running local baseline vision model training.

### 5.2 Gate 2 (Tranche 3 Release Criteria - Month 11)
* **MS-2.1**: Initial batch of 1,000 Bhumika-1 nodes pass SMT automated optical inspection (AOI) with >99% pass rate.
* **MS-2.2**: 10 KR-Alpha rovers execute path-following autonomous steering within ±5cm accuracy.
* **MS-2.3**: HIL firmware test rig running 100% automated continuous integration suites.

## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra’s 5-year financial plan reflects rapid transition from hardware R&D in Year 1 to high-margin SaaS and PAaaS expansion in Years 2–5 across Indian agricultural regions.

Financial Summary:
* **Year 1**: **₹38.0 Lakhs** Revenue (Seed Round: ₹1.60 Crore injection; Net Cash End Y1: ₹13.28 Lakhs).
* **Year 2**: **₹3.80 Crores** Revenue (EBITDA positive by Q4 Year 2).
* **Year 3**: **₹17.50 Crores** Revenue (Series A round: ₹12.0 Crores raised for commercial scale).
* **Year 4**: **₹54.00 Crores** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 26.7%).
* **Year 5**: **₹150.00 Crores** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 33.9%; ARR: ₹118.0 Crores).

## 2. Core Financial Assumptions

```
[ Revenue Growth Drivers ]
- Year 1: 3 FPO/Cooperative Pilots (3,000 total acres)
- Year 2: 25,000 PAaaS acres + 50 Sanchay-1 storage units
- Year 3: 120,000 PAaaS acres + Enterprise Agri-OS Launch + Series A
- Year 4: 400,000 PAaaS acres + Regional Expansion
- Year 5: 1,200,000 PAaaS acres + Commodity Exchange Commission
```

* **Inflation Rate**: Assumed at 5.5% annually for hardware raw materials and logistics in India.
* **Corporate Tax Rate**: 25% applied after exhausting initial carry-forward tax losses under Income Tax Act.
* **Depreciation**: Straight-line method (5 years for equipment, 10 years for solar, 4 years for IT).
* **Payment Terms**: PAaaS billed annually in advance; Enterprise SaaS billed monthly.

## 9. Glossary
* **EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
* **COGS**: Cost of Goods Sold; the direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold in a company.
* **Pro-Forma**: A method of calculating financial results using hypothetical numbers or future estimates.
* **ROE**: Return on Equity; a measure of financial performance calculated by dividing net income by shareholders' equity.
* **FPO**: Farmer Producer Organization; a legal entity formed by primary producers/farmers in India.

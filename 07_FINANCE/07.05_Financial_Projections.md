## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra’s 5-year financial plan reflects rapid transition from tech campus construction in Year 1 to high-margin SaaS and PAaaS expansion across India and emerging export markets in Years 2–5.

Financial Summary:
* **Year 1**: **₹8.50 Crores** Revenue (Seed Round: ₹32.00 Crores injection; Net Cash End Y1: ₹8.50 Crores).
* **Year 2**: **₹45.00 Crores** Revenue (EBITDA positive by Q3 Year 2 at ₹4.20 Crores).
* **Year 3**: **₹180.00 Crores** Revenue (Series A round: ₹120.00 Crores raised for national scale).
* **Year 4**: **₹550.00 Crores** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 27.2%).
* **Year 5**: **₹1,500.00 Crores** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 34.8%; ARR: ₹1,180 Crores).

## 2. Core Financial Assumptions

```
[ Revenue Growth Drivers ]
- Year 1: 5 FPO/Cooperative Pilots across 50,000 acres + 200 Storage retrofits
- Year 2: 250,000 PAaaS acres + 1,000 Storage Retrofits
- Year 3: 1,000,000 PAaaS acres + Enterprise Agri-OS Launch + Series A
- Year 4: 3,500,000 PAaaS acres + Regional Export (South Asia / SE Asia)
- Year 5: 10,000,000+ PAaaS acres + Commodity Exchange Commission
```

* **Inflation Rate**: Assumed at 5.5% annually for hardware raw materials and logistics in India.
* **Corporate Tax Rate**: 25% applied after exhausting initial carry-forward tax losses under Income Tax Act.
* **Depreciation**: Straight-line method (7 years for robotics/lab gear, 10 years for solar, 4 years for AI compute).
* **Payment Terms**: PAaaS billed annually in advance; Enterprise SaaS billed monthly.

## 9. Glossary
* **EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
* **COGS**: Cost of Goods Sold; direct costs attributable to production.
* **Pro-Forma**: Method of calculating financial results using hypothetical future estimates.
* **ROE**: Return on Equity; financial performance calculated as net income divided by shareholders' equity.

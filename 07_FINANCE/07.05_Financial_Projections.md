## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra’s 5-year financial plan reflects rapid transition from hardware R&D in Year 1 to high-margin SaaS and PAaaS expansion in Years 2–5.

Financial Summary:
* **Year 1**: **$45,000** Revenue (Seed Round: $190,000 injection; Net Cash End Y1: $25,000).
* **Year 2**: **$450,000** Revenue (EBITDA positive by Q4 Year 2).
* **Year 3**: **$2,100,000** Revenue (Series A round: $1.5M raised for commercial scale).
* **Year 4**: **$6,500,000** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 22%).
* **Year 5**: **$18,000,000** Revenue (Net Profit Margin: 28%; ARR: $14.2M).

## 2. Core Financial Assumptions

```
[ Revenue Growth Drivers ]
- Year 1: 3 Cooperative Pilots (3,000 total acres)
- Year 2: 25,000 PAaaS acres + 50 Sanchay-1 warehouse units
- Year 3: 120,000 PAaaS acres + Enterprise Agri-OS Launch + Series A
- Year 4: 400,000 PAaaS acres + International Beta Launch
- Year 5: 1,200,000 PAaaS acres + Commodity Exchange Commission
```

* **Inflation Rate**: Assumed at 5% annually for hardware raw materials and logistics.
* **Corporate Tax Rate**: 25% applied after exhausting initial carry-forward tax losses.
* **Depreciation**: Straight-line method (5 years for equipment, 10 years for solar, 4 years for IT).
* **Payment Terms**: PAaaS billed annually in advance; Enterprise SaaS billed monthly.

## 9. Glossary
* **EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
* **COGS**: Cost of Goods Sold; the direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold in a company.
* **Pro-Forma**: A method of calculating financial results using hypothetical numbers or future estimates.
* **ROE**: Return on Equity; a measure of financial performance calculated by dividing net income by shareholders' equity.

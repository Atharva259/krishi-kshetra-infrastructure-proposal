## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document details the 5-year audited financial model, income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow projections for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) based on securing **₹50.00 Crores** in institutional seed funding under a 1-year land purchase, civil campus building construction, and technology deployment timeline.

### Scope
This model covers the financial trajectory from Year 1 through Year 5:
* **Year 1 (Land Purchase & Building Construction Phase)**: **₹0 Revenue** (100% focused on land acquisition, civil construction of a 25,000 sq. ft. campus, solar grid installation, software/firmware R&D; OPEX Burn: **₹20.00 Crores**).
* **Year 2 (Facility Launch & Commercial Pilot)**: **₹4.50 Crores Revenue** (First 20,000 acres Precision Agriculture as a Service [PAaaS] + 100 cold storage retrofits; Net Loss: **₹8.50 Crores**).
* **Year 3 (Commercial Scale)**: **₹28.00 Crores Revenue** (120,000 acres PAaaS + Agri-OS SaaS; **EBITDA Positive**; Net Profit: **₹4.50 Crores**; Series A of **₹100 Crores**).
* **Year 4 (Regional Scale)**: **₹95.00 Crores Revenue** (450,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit: **₹22.00 Crores**).
* **Year 5 (National Expansion)**: **₹260.00 Crores Revenue** (1,200,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit: **₹72.00 Crores**).

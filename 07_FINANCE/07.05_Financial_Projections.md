## 1. Executive Summary

Krishi Kshetra’s conservative 5-year financial plan reflects a grounded, disciplined transition from tech campus construction in Year 1 to steady SaaS and PAaaS expansion across India in Years 2–5.

Conservative Financial Summary:
* **Year 1**: **₹1.20 Crores** Revenue (Seed Round: ₹32.00 Crores injection; 5,000-acre pilot deployment; Net Cash End Y1: ₹4.00 Crores).
* **Year 2**: **₹6.50 Crores** Revenue (30,000 acres PAaaS + 100 storage retrofits).
* **Year 3**: **₹25.00 Crores** Revenue (120,000 acres PAaaS; **EBITDA positive** at ₹5.20 Crores; Series A round: ₹60.00 Crores).
* **Year 4**: **₹75.00 Crores** Revenue (400,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit Margin: 22.0%).
* **Year 5**: **₹200.00 Crores** Revenue (1,000,000 acres PAaaS; Net Profit Margin: 26.0%; ARR: ₹155 Crores).

## 2. Core Financial Assumptions

```
[ Revenue Growth Drivers ]
- Year 1: 5,000 acres PAaaS pilot + 20 Sanchay-1 storage retrofits
- Year 2: 30,000 acres PAaaS + 100 Sanchay-1 storage retrofits
- Year 3: 120,000 acres PAaaS + Enterprise Agri-OS Cloud SaaS launch + Series A
- Year 4: 400,000 acres PAaaS + regional expansion
- Year 5: 1,000,000 acres PAaaS + national footprint
```

* **Inflation Rate**: Assumed at 5.5% annually for hardware raw materials and logistics in India.
* **Corporate Tax Rate**: 25% applied after exhausting initial carry-forward tax losses under Income Tax Act.
* **Depreciation**: Straight-line method (7 years for robotics/lab gear, 10 years for solar, 4 years for AI compute).
* **Payment Terms**: PAaaS billed annually in advance; Enterprise SaaS billed monthly.

## 6. Break-Even Analysis

```
[ Monthly Fixed Costs ] : ₹1.25 Crores / month (Year 3 Average)
[ Average Contribution Margin ] : 66%
[ Monthly Break-Even Revenue ] : ₹1.25 Crores / 0.66 = ₹1.89 Crores / month
```

* **Target Break-Even Date**: Month 30 (Q2 Year 3).
* **Break-Even Volume**: 110,000 active PAaaS subscribed acres or 170 Sanchay-1 storage retrofits per month.

## 9. Glossary
* **EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization.
* **COGS**: Cost of Goods Sold; direct costs attributable to production.
* **Pro-Forma**: Method of calculating financial results using hypothetical future estimates.
* **ROE**: Return on Equity; financial performance calculated as net income divided by shareholders' equity.

## 📊 Growth Strategy

The **₹120.00 Crore** master envelope is not a standard software seed round; it is a massive foundational capital expenditure designed to permanently eliminate OpEx overheads (cloud, manufacturing outsourcing, real estate) from years 3 through 10.

## 💰 Projection Assumptions

### 1. Revenue Deferred for Hardware Moat (Months 1 - 36)
We project minimal commercial revenue during the first 36 months. The ₹15.00 Crore Extended OPEX Runway fully subsidizes engineering compensation, facility utilities, and mobile data (Airtel/Jio) during this intensive R&D phase. 

### 2. Zero Cloud Compute Scaling Costs
Because we have heavily capitalized our own compute via single-tank immersion clusters and 15x RTX 5090 workstations, our cost-of-goods-sold (COGS) for training the AI Crop Advisor drops to near-zero. Competitors scaling on AWS will face exponentially growing server bills; our margins remain flat regardless of data scale.

### 3. Vertical Hardware Margins (Months 36 - 60)
When the robotic fleet enters mass production, we will manufacture them utilizing the in-house 5-axis CNC and SMT pick-and-place lines (procured for ₹3.50 Cr). Retaining manufacturing sovereignty ensures profit margins on physical hardware exceed 60%, drastically outperforming outsourced models.

### 4. Microgrid Utility Recoup
The 2.5 MW solar PV farm and BESS storage (₹12.00 Cr) eliminates commercial power costs for the manufacturing floor, effectively amortizing the investment within 7 years while guaranteeing zero downtime.

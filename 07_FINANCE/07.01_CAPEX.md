## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 25,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, deploy a 500 kVA Silent Industrial Diesel Generator for campus power, procure compact portable silent inverter generators, portable wireless printers, card/document scanners, foldable field desks, Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

## 3. Portable Mobile Field Office Breakdown

To process farmer registrations, scan government identity cards, print soil telemetry reports, and set up outdoor field desks in off-grid rural villages:
* **Mobile Field Office Suite (₹0.20 Crores)**:
  * **Portable Wireless Battery Printers (Epson WF-110 / Canon TR150)**: Compact, lightweight battery-powered wireless color printers.
  * **High-Speed Duplex Document & ID Card Scanners (Brother DS-940DW / Fujitsu ScanSnap iX100)**: Portable sheet-fed scanners with dedicated ID card slots for scanning Aadhaar cards, Kisan Credit Cards, land deeds, and contracts right inside XUV 7XO SUVs or at outdoor desks.
  * **Foldable Aluminium Field Desks & Ergonomic Chairs**: Lightweight, heavy-duty folding aluminum tables, ergonomic camp chairs, and pop-up rain/sun canopies for rapid 5-minute field booth deployment.

## 4. Glossary
* **ID Card Scanner**: Compact portable sheet-fed scanner equipped with a dedicated plastic card slot for instant identification verification.
* **Portable Wireless Printer**: Battery-operated mobile printer producing hardcopy contracts and reports in the field.

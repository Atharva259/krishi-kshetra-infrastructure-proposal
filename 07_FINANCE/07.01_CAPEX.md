## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, and equip the labs, desktop builds, and laptops for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 12-month construction and setup window under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round. This includes land plot purchase, civil building construction, **₹4.00 Crores in high-end Desktop Workstation builds** (RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs, AIO liquid cooling), **₹3.00 Crores in Flagship Laptops**, industrial SMT manufacturing lines, 5-axis CNC milling centers, solar energy microgrids, IT network backbones, and component inventories.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. 

This investment covers:
* **Land & Civil Building Construction**: **₹7.00 Crores** (Land plot purchase + custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus construction).
* **High-End Desktop Workstation Builds**: **₹4.00 Crores** (Multi-GPU RTX 5090/5080 rigs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs, AIO liquid cooling, dual 4K displays for core employees and AI research).
* **Engineering & Field Laptops**: **₹3.00 Crores** (Flagship MacBook Pro M3 Max / Mobile Workstations for engineering leads, agronomists & field ops).
* **Robotics & Industrial Labs**: **₹3.50 Crores** (Yamaha SMT line, Haas 5-axis CNC mill, SLS/SLA 3D printers, RF anechoic chamber).
* **Solar Microgrid & Energy Infrastructure**: **₹1.50 Crores** (250kW solar PV array + 500kWh battery storage bank).
* **Component Stock & Mobile Test Fleet**: **₹1.00 Crore** (Testing mobile units, ZFS NAS, and silicon pre-orders).

## 4. Glossary
* **Desktop Builds**: Custom-configured high-performance PC workstations with AIO liquid cooling and flagship GPUs.
* **AIO Cooling**: All-In-One liquid cooling loops for CPUs and GPUs.
* **RTX 5090 / 5080**: Flagship graphics processing units optimized for AI deep learning.
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used to acquire physical assets.

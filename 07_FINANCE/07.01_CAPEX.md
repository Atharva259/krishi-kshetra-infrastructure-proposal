## 3. Portable Village Advertising Silent Generators Breakdown

To power high-decibel outdoor farmer rallies, mobile LED projection screens, 5,000W concert PA sound systems, and video filming in off-grid rural villages:
* **4 Portable Ultra-Silent Inverter Generator Sets (₹0.30 Crores)**:
  * Honda EU70is / Yamaha EF6300iS 7.5 kVA Portable Silent Inverter Generators.
  * Compact form factor with fold-down transport handles and heavy-duty puncture-proof wheels; fits directly into the rear boot cargo space of our Mahindra XUV 7XO SUVs.
  * Advanced Inverter Technology delivering Clean Pure Sine Wave AC power (<1% Total Harmonic Distortion) to safely power sensitive electronics, laptops, 4K laser projectors, and Sony FX6/FX3 cinema camera battery stations.
  * Eco-Throttle acoustic soundproofing sound levels as low as 52 dBA at 7 meters.

## 4. Glossary
* **Portable Inverter Generator**: Compact, hand-wheeled silent generator producing pure sine wave AC electricity, designed to fit inside an SUV boot for off-grid field roadshows.
* **Pure Sine Wave**: High-quality AC power output safe for sensitive computers, laser projectors, and cinema cameras.

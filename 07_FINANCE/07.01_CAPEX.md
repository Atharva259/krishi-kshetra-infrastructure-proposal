## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, procure Mahindra XUV700 AWD field SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round:
* **Land Plot Acquisition & Permanent Civil Campus Construction**: ₹6.50 Crores.
* **Temporary First Office Renovation, Electrical Rewiring & Furniture**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **500kVA Industrial HT Transformer Substation**: ₹1.00 Crore.

* **Concert-Grade PA Sound Systems, Subwoofers & Digital Mixers**: ₹0.40 Crores.
* **GaN Fast Chargers, Power Banks & Vehicle 230V Inverters**: ₹0.20 Crores.
* **High-Lumen 4K Laser Projectors & 86" Interactive Displays**: ₹0.60 Crores.
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds (RTX 5090/5080)**: ₹3.00 Crores.
* **High-Performance Gaming Laptops Fleet (ASUS ROG / Razer / MSI)**: ₹2.00 Crores.
* **Unlisted Corporate Studio & Cinema Camera Fleet (Studio + Drones)**: ₹1.00 Crore.

## 4. Concert PA Sound Systems & High-Wattage Charging Suite

To power high-volume outdoor farmer talks and keep laptop fleets charged anywhere:
* **Concert-Grade PA Sound Systems (₹0.40 Crores)**: Dual 5,000W active line-array speaker towers, active subwoofers, Behringer X32 digital mixers, and wireless mic systems for rural field rallies and trade shows.
* **GaN Fast Chargers & Vehicle Inverters (₹0.20 Crores)**: 240W/140W GaN desktop chargers, 25,000mAh 100W PD power banks, charging cabinets, and 230V pure sine wave inverters installed in XUV700 vehicles to charge ASUS ROG / Razer gaming laptops on location.

## 5. Glossary
* **Mahindra XUV700 AWD**: Flagship all-wheel-drive SUV built for high-speed highway travel and rugged rural off-road tracks.
* **GaN Fast Charger**: Gallium Nitride power charger delivering high wattage (140W-240W) in compact form factor for gaming laptops.
* **Line-Array PA System**: Concert-grade directional loudspeaker array designed for outdoor public address and clear speech intelligibility.

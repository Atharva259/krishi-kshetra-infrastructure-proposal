## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, and equip the labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 12-month construction and setup window under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round. This includes land plot purchase, civil building construction, SMT manufacturing lines, 5-axis CNC milling centers, NVIDIA AI compute nodes, solar energy microgrids, IT network backbones, and component inventories.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. 

Rather than leasing commercial space, **₹8.00 Crores is allocated to purchasing a land plot and constructing a custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus facility over a 12-month period**. The remaining ₹12.00 Crores of CAPEX is dedicated to equipping the SMT manufacturing line, 5-axis CNC machining center, 250kW solar microgrid, NVIDIA AI compute cluster, and component inventory during the buildout phase.

## 2. CAPEX Objectives & 1-Year Construction Timeline

```
[ Months 1 - 3 ]  ──> [ Months 4 - 8 ] ──> [ Months 9 - 12 ]
• Land Acquisition     • Civil Building      • SMT, CNC & AI Compute Setup
• Permits & Design     • Construction & ESD  • Solar Microgrid Commissioning
```

### 2.1 1-Year Civil Construction Execution Schedule:
* **Months 1 – 3 (Land Acquisition & Design)**:
  * Finalize land plot purchase and complete property title registration.
  * Obtain municipal civil construction, environmental, and electrical grid connection permits.
  * Finalize architectural blueprints for ESD laboratory floors, heavy machinery foundations, and server vaults.
* **Months 4 – 8 (Civil Campus Building Construction)**:
  * Construct the 15,000 sq. ft. two-story facility shell and interior partitions.
  * Lay heavy concrete foundations for 5-axis CNC machines and install anti-static ESD flooring.
* **Months 9 – 12 (Machinery Commissioning & Solar Setup)**:
  * Mount the 250kW rooftop solar PV array and commissioning 500kWh battery storage bank.
  * Deliver, assemble, and calibrate Yamaha YSM20R SMT line, Haas CNC mills, and NVIDIA AI compute cluster.

## 5. Glossary
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used to acquire long-term physical assets.
* **EPC Contractor**: Engineering, Procurement, and Construction contractor responsible for building projects.
* **ESD**: Electrostatic Discharge; measures to prevent damage to sensitive microelectronics during assembly.
* **LiFePO4**: Lithium Iron Phosphate; stable battery chemistry offering long cycle life and high thermal safety.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. 

This investment covers:
* **Land & Civil Building Construction**: **₹7.00 Crores** (Land plot purchase + custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus construction).
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds**: **₹4.00 Crores** (Custom AIO liquid cooling, RTX 5090/5080 GPUs, Threadripper/i9 CPUs, dual 4K displays for 11 core employees and AI research).
* **High-Performance Gaming Laptops**: **₹3.00 Crores** (ASUS ROG Strix SCAR 18, Razer Blade 18, MSI Raider GE78 for portable GPU compute, field simulations, and team leads).
* **Robotics & Industrial Labs**: **₹3.50 Crores** (Yamaha SMT line, Haas 5-axis CNC mill, SLS/SLA 3D printers, RF anechoic chamber).
* **Solar Microgrid & Energy Infrastructure**: **₹1.50 Crores** (250kW solar PV array + 500kWh battery storage bank).
* **Component Stock & Mobile Test Fleet**: **₹1.00 Crore** (Testing mobile units, ZFS NAS, and silicon pre-orders).

## 3. Justification of Gaming Laptops & Custom AIO Builds

### 3.1 Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds
* **Thermal Dissipation**: High-end Threadripper/i9 CPUs paired with RTX 5090/5080 GPUs draw up to 800W per rig. 360mm Custom AIO liquid cooling loops ensure sustained boost clocks without thermal throttling during multi-hour neural net training.
* **Reliability**: AIO liquid loops prevent dust buildup in dusty agricultural laboratory environments.

### 3.2 High-Performance Gaming Laptops
* **High TGP Mobile GPUs**: High-end gaming laptops (ASUS ROG Strix SCAR 18, Razer Blade 18, MSI Raider) deliver up to 175W TGP (Total Graphics Power) on mobile RTX 4090/5090 GPUs, making them portable AI supercomputers for field testing.
* **Vapor Chamber Cooling**: Ensures high sustained performance during outdoors field trials under direct sunlight.

## 4. Glossary
* **Custom AIO Builds**: Custom desktop workstations equipped with All-In-One closed-loop liquid coolers.
* **Gaming Laptops**: High-performance mobile laptops featuring high-TGP discrete GPUs and vapor chamber cooling.
* **RTX 5090 / 5080**: Flagship graphics processing units optimized for AI deep learning.
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used to acquire physical assets.

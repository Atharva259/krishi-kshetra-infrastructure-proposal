### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, procure Mahindra XUV700 AX7 Luxury Pack AWD SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to establish the 15,000 sq. ft. operational, engineering, prototyping, and manufacturing campus for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under a 90-day fast-track buildout plan.

This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 90-day construction window. This includes industrial laboratory instrumentation, SMT manufacturing lines, 5-axis CNC milling centers, NVIDIA AI compute nodes, solar energy microgrids, IT network backbones, and component inventories.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. 

This investment is structured to construct an industrial-grade mechatronics, AI supercomputing, and SMT assembly campus in **under 90 days** in India. This rapid workspace construction eliminates vendor dependencies, reduces product iteration cycles to hours, and supports low-volume mass production of 10,000 telemetry nodes and 100 autonomous rovers.

## 2. CAPEX Objectives & 90-Day Buildout Timeline

```
[ Days 1 - 30 ]  ──> [ Days 31 - 60 ] ──> [ Days 61 - 90 ]
• Civil & ESD Flooring • SMT Line & CNC Setup • AI Server Room Commissioning
• 250kW Solar Mounting • Anechoic RF Chamber   • HIL & Device Farm QA Setup
```

### 2.1 90-Day Fast-Track Workspace Execution Schedule:
* **Days 1 – 30 (Site Preparation & Renewable Power)**:
  * Finalize 15,000 sq. ft. industrial campus lease.
  * Install anti-static ESD flooring and continuous grounding tracks.
  * Mount 250kW rooftop solar PV array and commissioning 500kWh battery storage bank.
* **Days 31 – 60 (Industrial Machinery & Prototyping Setup)**:
  * Deliver, assemble, and calibrate Yamaha YSM20R high-speed SMT pick-and-place line.
  * Install Haas UMC-750 5-axis CNC milling machines and RF anechoic test chamber.
* **Days 61 – 90 (AI Compute & Production Launch)**:
  * Commission NVIDIA HGX AI compute server room with 100GbE fiber switching.
  * Deploy 100+ unit Android Go mobile device QA farm and HIL microcontroller test rigs.
  * Commence production of first 1,000 Bhumika-1 nodes and 10 KR-Alpha rovers.

## 5. Glossary
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used to acquire and maintain physical assets.
* **SMT**: Surface Mount Technology; mounting electronic components directly onto printed circuit boards.
* **CNC**: Computer Numerical Control; automated precision control of machining tools.
* **HIL**: Hardware-in-the-Loop; automated testing of embedded software against physical electronics.
* **LiFePO4**: Lithium Iron Phosphate; stable battery chemistry offering long cycle life and high thermal safety.

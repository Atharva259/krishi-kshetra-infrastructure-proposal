## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to establish the operational, engineering, prototyping, and manufacturing campus for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores). It outlines the procurement strategy, vendor evaluation frameworks, asset lifetime amortizations, and risk mitigation strategies for our **₹32 Crore** funding round.

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 18-month deployment window. This includes industrial laboratory instrumentation, SMT manufacturing lines, 5-axis CNC milling centers, NVIDIA AI compute nodes, solar energy microgrids, IT network backbones, and component inventories.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. The remaining ₹12.00 Crores is allocated to operational working capital and engineering personnel salaries. 

This investment is structured to build an industrial-grade mechatronics, AI supercomputing, and SMT assembly campus in India that eliminates vendor dependencies, reduces product iteration cycles to hours, and supports low-volume mass production of 10,000 telemetry nodes and 100 autonomous rovers.

---

## 2. CAPEX Objectives
1. **Industrial Manufacturing Independence**: Full vertical integration of PCB SMT manufacturing, 5-axis CNC mechanical milling, and firmware testing inside our campus.
2. **Campus Energy Self-Sufficiency**: Deploy a 250kW rooftop solar microgrid and 500kWh battery storage system to insulate servers, SMT ovens, and AI compute nodes from grid interruptions.
3. **On-Premise AI Supercomputing**: Install dedicated NVIDIA HGX compute nodes to fine-tune custom computer vision and agronomic multimodal models locally.
4. **HIL & Device Farm QA Infrastructure**: Establish Hardware-in-the-Loop test setups and a 100+ unit mobile device farm (including Android Go units) for end-to-end software quality assurance.

## 4. Justification of Engineering & Hardware Decisions

### 4.1 Industrial SMT Line vs. Outsourced Board Assembly
Outsourcing PCB production for 10,000 nodes to external assembly plants entails high setup fees and minimum order delays of 4 to 6 weeks. Operating our Yamaha YSM20R SMT line gives us:
* **Production Rate**: Up to 45,000 components placed per hour.
* **Unit Cost Savings**: Reduces PCB manufacturing cost per node from ₹2,500 (outsourced) to ₹850 (in-house).
* **IP Protection**: Prevents circuit design leaks prior to global patent grants.

### 4.2 On-Premise AI Compute vs. Pure Cloud GPU Instances
Renting NVIDIA H100 GPUs on cloud providers costs approximately ₹250 to ₹350 per GPU hour. Fine-tuning our computer vision models on millions of agricultural images requires thousands of compute hours per month. Owning an NVIDIA HGX node pays for itself within **14 months** while maintaining zero data transfer latency.

## 7. CAPEX Risk Analysis & Mitigations

### 7.1 Supply Chain Delays
* **Risk**: Lead time spikes on microcontrollers (STM32) and RTK-GPS modules delay mechatronics assembly.
* **Mitigation**: Deploy ₹5.00 Crores immediately in Category D component inventory upon seed fund closing, securing silicon for 10,000 nodes directly from authorized domestic distributors.

### 7.2 Equipment Failure
* **Risk**: SMT reflow line or 5-axis CNC mill breakdown halts production.
* **Mitigation**: Maintenance contracts with Haas India and Yamaha SMT representatives, including 24-hour on-site technician SLAs.

## 10. Glossary
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used by a company to acquire and maintain physical assets.
* **SMT**: Surface Mount Technology; mounting electronic components directly onto printed circuit boards.
* **CNC**: Computer Numerical Control; automated precision control of machining tools.
* **HIL**: Hardware-in-the-Loop; automated testing of embedded software against physical electronics.
* **LiFePO4**: Lithium Iron Phosphate; stable battery chemistry offering long cycle life and high thermal safety.

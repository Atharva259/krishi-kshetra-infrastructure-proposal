## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 25,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, establish an Executive Investor Boardroom with a 110" MicroLED 4K Display Wall, procure industrial SMT & CNC manufacturing machinery, build a media production studio, deploy a DJI Inspire 3 8K Enterprise Cinema Drone Fleet for large-area farm recording, deploy a 500 kVA Silent Industrial Diesel Generator, procure portable silent inverter generators, portable wireless printers, card/document scanners, foldable field desks, Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics/agronomy testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required under our **₹50.00 Crore** seed funding round:
* **Land Plot Acquisition & Permanent Civil Campus Construction (25,000 sq. ft.)**: ₹10.00 Crores.

* **Executive Investor Boardroom Suite & 110" MicroLED / 86" Display Wall**: ₹0.80 Crores.

* **Media Production Studio, Cinema Rigs & DJI 8K Enterprise Drone Suite**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds (RTX 5090/5080 Rigs)**: ₹3.60 Crores.

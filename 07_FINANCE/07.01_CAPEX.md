## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 25,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, establish a dedicated 1,500 sq. ft. Executive Investor Boardroom & Command Center with a 110" MicroLED 4K Display Wall, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, deploy a 500 kVA Silent Industrial Diesel Generator, procure portable silent inverter generators, portable wireless printers, card/document scanners, foldable field desks, Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics/agronomy testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

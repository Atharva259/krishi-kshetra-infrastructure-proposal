## 3. Structural Metal Chassis & 3D Body Engineering Details

* **Structural Metal Chassis Line (₹0.70 Crores)**: Precision 5-axis CNC milling of 6061-T6 aircraft aluminum chassis billets, CNC tube bending for protective tubular steel roll cages, TIG/MIG welding stations, and an anodizing dip tank to produce lightweight, corrosion-proof metal robot frames.
* **Industrial 3D Printed Body Production Line (₹0.50 Crores)**: High-speed large-format additive manufacturing printers using Markforged Onyx (micro-carbon fiber reinforced nylon) and UV-stabilized ASA/PC thermoplastics to print aerodynamic rover body covers, IP67 waterproof camera enclosures, battery bay covers, and drone shells.

---

## 4. Glossary
* **Metal Chassis Fabrication**: CNC milling, bending, welding, and anodizing of structural 6061-T6 aluminum frames.
* **3D Printed Outer Body**: Additive manufacturing of UV-stabilized, carbon fiber reinforced outer enclosures for agricultural rovers and drones.

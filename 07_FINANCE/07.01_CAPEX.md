## 3. 500 kVA Silent Industrial Generator Breakdown

To maintain 100% uptime for multi-GPU AI servers, custom AIO workstations, 5-axis CNC mills, SMT PCB assembly lines, and soundproof studio lighting during power grid failures:
* **500 kVA Cummins / Caterpillar Heavy-Duty DG Set (₹0.80 Crores)**:
  * 3-Phase 415V 500 kVA diesel generator engine.
  * AMF (Auto Mains Failure) Panel for instant automatic transfer within 3 seconds of grid loss.
  * Soundproof acoustic canopy adhering to CPCB Class II noise emission norms (<75 dBA at 1 meter).
  * 1,000-liter bulk diesel day tank with automated fuel monitoring.

---

## 4. Glossary
* **500 kVA Silent DG Set**: Heavy-duty industrial diesel generator housed inside a soundproof acoustic enclosure with automatic mains failure switching.
* **AMF Panel**: Automatic Mains Failure panel that senses grid outage and automatically fires up the backup generator.

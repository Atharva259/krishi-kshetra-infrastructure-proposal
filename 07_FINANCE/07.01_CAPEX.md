## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's initial CAPEX budget is established at **₹1.00 Crore (₹100.0 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹1.60 Crore (₹160.0 Lakhs)** seed capital request. The remaining ₹60.0 Lakhs is allocated to operational working capital and engineering salaries. 

This investment is structured to build a highly functional mechatronics and software prototyping environment in India that reduces dependencies on external vendors, limits long-term product iteration cycles, and establishes complete operational readiness within 12 months.

---

## 2. CAPEX Objectives
1. **Rapid Prototyping Independence**: Complete vertical integration of PCB manufacturing, mechanical fabrication, and firmware testing inside our proprietary labs.
2. **Operational Continuity**: Implement a robust 15kW solar microgrid and 30kWh battery backup system to insulate computing and test equipment from local grid outages.
3. **Enterprise-Grade Security & Network Architecture**: Deploy secure, locally partitioned networks to safeguard proprietary AI algorithms, datasets, and firmware.
4. **HIL Testing Infrastructure**: Establish Hardware-in-the-Loop test setups and mobile device farms (including Android Go units) to assure software quality across multiple platforms prior to field deployment.

## 4. Justification of Engineering & Hardware Decisions

### 4.1 In-house Prototyping vs. External Outsource
Outsourcing PCB production and mechanical CNC milling typically requires a 7 to 15-day turnaround time per iteration. In agricultural deployments, where environmental factors change rapidly, hardware errors must be corrected immediately. Establishing our own Pick-and-Place line (Charmhigh CHMT48VB) and Desktop CNC (Pocket NC) allows us to design, assemble, and test firmware changes in a single day, reducing product time-to-market by **80%**.

### 4.2 LiFePO4 vs. Standard Lithium-Ion Batteries
For both our solar microgrid battery storage (EG4) and autonomous rovers, standard Lithium-Ion (NMC) chemistries represent a thermal risk. Agricultural environments experience operating temperatures up to 50°C. 
Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery chemistry provides:
* **Thermal Runway Safety**: LiFePO4 remains stable up to 60°C.
* **Extended Service Lifespan**: Up to 3,000 charge cycles compared to 500 cycles for standard Lithium-Ion. This reduces long-term maintenance costs.

### 7.1 Supply Chain Delays
* **Risk**: Long lead times on microcontrollers (STM32) and RTK-GPS components delay mechatronics assembly.
* **Mitigation**: Pre-order standard silicon components (Category D) immediately upon receipt of seed funding. Source from authorized domestic distributors with guaranteed delivery dates.

### 7.2 Equipment Failure
* **Risk**: CNC mill or SLA printer failure halts prototype production.
* **Mitigation**: Maintain service agreements with local technical representatives. Ensure redundant FDM printers (Prusa i3) are available for non-critical parts.

### 7.3 Grid Power Fluctuations
* **Risk**: Power surges or sudden outages damage local compilation servers or reflow ovens.
* **Mitigation**: Install hybrid inverters with automatic transfer switches (ATS) that isolate laboratory equipment from the public grid, keeping them on solar battery backup.

## 10. Glossary
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used by a company to acquire, upgrade, and maintain physical assets.
* **SMT**: Surface Mount Technology; a method for producing electronic circuits in which components are mounted directly onto the surface of printed circuit boards (PCBs).
* **CNC**: Computer Numerical Control; automated control of machining tools using microcomputers.
* **HIL**: Hardware-in-the-Loop; a technique used in the development and test of complex real-time embedded systems.
* **ZFS**: Zettabyte File System; a file system and logical volume manager designed to protect data against corruption.
* **LiFePO4**: Lithium Iron Phosphate; a stable battery chemistry offering long cycle life and high thermal safety.

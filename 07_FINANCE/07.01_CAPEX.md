## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, and equip the labs and IT infrastructure for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 12-month construction and setup window under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round. This includes land plot purchase, civil building construction, SMT manufacturing lines, 5-axis CNC milling centers, NVIDIA HGX AI compute nodes, RTX 5090/5080 multi-GPU workstation rigs, high-end AIO liquid-cooled CPUs for 11 core employees, engineering laptops, testing mobile devices, solar energy microgrids, IT network backbones, and component inventories.

## 1. Executive Summary
Krishi Kshetra's CAPEX budget is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **62.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed capital request. 

This investment covers:
* **Land & Civil Building Construction**: **₹8.00 Crores** (Land plot purchase + custom 15,000 sq. ft. campus construction).
* **Robotics & Industrial Labs**: **₹5.50 Crores** (Yamaha SMT line, Haas 5-axis CNC mill, SLS/SLA 3D printers, RF anechoic chamber).
* **Solar Microgrid & Energy**: **₹2.50 Crores** (250kW solar PV array + 500kWh battery storage bank).
* **Computing, Workstations & Silicon Inventory**: **₹4.00 Crores** (NVIDIA HGX AI node, RTX 5090/5080 multi-GPU workstations, high-end AIO liquid-cooled PCs for 11 core employees, engineering laptops, mobile test units, and silicon pre-orders).

## 3. Justification of Workstation & GPU Specifications

### 3.1 RTX 5090 / RTX 5080 Flagship GPUs & AIO Liquid Cooling
Computer vision models for crop disease identification require massive parallel matrix operations. Deploys:
* **RTX 5090 / 5080 GPUs**: Next-gen Ada/Blackwell architecture with high VRAM capacity, allowing local inference testing without cloud latency.
* **AIO Liquid Cooling**: 360mm All-in-One liquid cooling loops prevent thermal throttling during multi-hour neural network training and CAD rendering runs.

### 3.2 11 Core Employee Workstation & Mobile Deployment
Each of the 11 core base employees is provisioned with a dual-system setup:
1. **Desktop Station**: High-end multi-core CPU, 64GB DDR5 ECC RAM, dual 4K color-calibrated monitors, and uninterruptible power supply (UPS).
2. **Mobile Laptop**: Flagship M3 Max MacBook Pro / Dell Precision for client meetings, field deployment tests, and remote collaboration.

## 5. Glossary
* **AIO Cooling**: All-In-One closed-loop liquid cooling system for high-performance CPUs and GPUs.
* **RTX 5090 / 5080**: Next-generation flagship graphics processing units optimized for AI deep learning.
* **CAPEX**: Capital Expenditures; funds used to acquire long-term physical assets.
* **ZFS**: Zettabyte File System; fault-tolerant enterprise storage file system.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, procure office equipment, apply for dedicated HT industrial transformers, and equip the labs, custom AIO desktop builds, and gaming laptops for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required during the initial 12-month construction and setup window under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round:
* **Land Plot Acquisition & Permanent Civil Campus Construction**: ₹6.50 Crores.
* **Temporary First Office Renovation, Electrical Rewiring & Equipment Buying**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **500kVA Industrial HT Transformer & Substation Infrastructure**: ₹1.00 Crore.
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds (RTX 5090/5080)**: ₹4.00 Crores.
* **High-Performance Gaming Laptops Fleet (ASUS ROG / Razer / MSI)**: ₹3.00 Crores.
* **Robotics & Industrial Labs (Haas 5-axis CNC, Yamaha SMT Line)**: ₹3.00 Crores.
* **Solar Microgrid (250kW) & 500kWh Battery Storage**: ₹1.00 Crore.

## 3. Temporary First Office Renovation & Electrical Rewiring

To launch software, firmware, and initial mechanical engineering on Day 1 while the permanent 15,000 sq. ft. campus is constructed:
* **Temporary Office Fit-Out (₹1.00 Crore)**: Renovation of a 3,500 sq. ft. temporary suite including dry-wall acoustic partitions, ESD antistatic mats, executive desks, ergonomic chairs, conference room AV systems, and HVAC units.
* **Industrial Electrical Rewiring (₹0.50 Crores)**: High-capacity copper busbars, isolated clean-grounding circuits for RTX 5090/5080 workstations, and 3-phase sub-panels.

---

## 4. Industrial 500kVA HT Transformer & Substation Application

To power multi-GPU AI servers, 5-axis CNC mills, and SMT lines:
* **State DISCOM Application & Security Deposit**: Formal application for an 11kV High-Tension (HT) commercial power connection.
* **500kVA Outdoor Substation Yard**: Installation of a 500kVA 11kV/433V step-down oil-cooled transformer, SF6 vacuum circuit breaker, HT metering kiosk, and automatic power factor correction (APFC) panel (**₹1.00 Crore**).

---

## 5. Glossary
* **HT Substation**: High-Tension electrical substation that steps down 11kV grid voltage to 433V 3-phase industrial power.
* **DISCOM**: Distribution Company (State Electricity Board).
* **ESD Rewiring**: Specialized electrical wiring with isolated earth grounding loops to protect sensitive microelectronics.

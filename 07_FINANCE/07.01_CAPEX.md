## 📄 Purpose & Scope

This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required for Krishi Kshetra's massive enterprise scaling. Shifting away from bloated external data center infrastructure, we are routing capital into a resilient, high-speed local development setup, robust multi-ISP network safety, advanced hardware labs, and high-performance workstation fleets.

The total CAPEX allocation is **₹105.00 Crores** (part of the total ₹120.00 Crore master envelope, which includes ₹15.00 Crores in OPEX).

## 🔒 Physical Security & Optical Camera Network

To protect high-security zones, the R&D labs, and the server/immersion enclosures, a complete commercial-grade optical surveillance system is integrated:
* **Perimeter & Entry Control**: 4K Sony Starvis optical security cameras with wide dynamic range (WDR) and infrared night vision.
* **Internal Corridors**: High-definition 5MP vandal-proof dome cameras with on-board person/vehicle analytics.
* **High-Security Server & R&D Vaults**: Biometric-gated entry logs paired with dedicated 4K PTZ cameras offering 24/7 continuous recording.
* **Storage**: Video streams piped over a dedicated segregated VLAN to enterprise-grade NVRs equipped with Seagate SkyHawk / Enterprise surveillance-optimized HDDs (RAID 6).

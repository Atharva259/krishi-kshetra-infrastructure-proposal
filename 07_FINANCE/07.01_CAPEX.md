## 3. Media Production Studio & DJI 8K Enterprise Drone Fleet (₹1.50 Crores)

To produce broadcast-quality marketing videos, technical reviews, outdoor agricultural field trip recordings, APMC mandi advertisements, and large-area farm aerial cinematography:

* **DJI Inspire 3 & Matrice 350 RTK 8K Enterprise Recording Drone Suite (₹0.40 Crores)**:
  * **DJI Inspire 3 Cinema Drone with Zenmuse X9-8K Air Gimbal**: Full-frame 8K ProRes RAW / CinemaDNG aerial camera with 15km O3 Pro transmission range and RTK centimeter positioning.
  * **DJI Matrice 350 RTK Enterprise Drone with Telephoto & Thermal Payload**: Heavy-duty long-range drone for recording large-scale 1,000+ acre farm trials, far-distance crop fields, and thermal crop health telemetry.
  * **Dual Ground Control Stations & Battery Charging Station**: Dual-operator controllers, 12 TB51 intelligent batteries, and high-gain antenna arrays for continuous field operation.

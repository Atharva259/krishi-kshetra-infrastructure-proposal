## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 15,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, procure 4x4 all-terrain field vehicles (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-lumen 4K projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores).

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding round:
* **Land Plot Acquisition & Permanent Civil Campus Construction**: ₹6.50 Crores.
* **Temporary First Office Renovation, Electrical Rewiring & Furniture**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **500kVA Industrial HT Transformer Substation**: ₹1.00 Crore.
* **4x4 All-Terrain Field Vehicles Fleet (4 Units @ ₹35 Lakhs each)**: ₹1.40 Crores.
* **High-Lumen 4K Laser Projectors & 86" Interactive Displays**: ₹0.60 Crores.
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds (RTX 5090/5080)**: ₹3.00 Crores.
* **High-Performance Gaming Laptops Fleet (ASUS ROG / Razer / MSI)**: ₹2.00 Crores.
* **Unlisted Corporate Studio & Cinema Camera Fleet (Studio + Drones + Field Recorders)**: ₹1.00 Crore.
* **Robotics, Electronic & Optical Lab Testing Equipment**: ₹1.00 Crore.
* **SMT PCB Assembly Line & 5-Axis CNC Mill**: ₹0.50 Crores.
* **250kW Solar Microgrid & 500kWh Battery Storage**: ₹0.50 Crores.

## 5. Glossary
* **4x4 Off-Road Vehicle**: Four-wheel-drive vehicle capable of navigating mud, unpaved tracks, and steep agricultural fields.
* **Laser Projector**: High-brightness solid-state laser projection system resistant to ambient outdoor light.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive, itemized breakdown of the Capital Expenditures (CAPEX) required to purchase land, construct a custom 25,000 sq. ft. technological facility over a 1-year construction period, renovate and rewire the temporary first office, build an unlisted corporate video production studio, procure Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs (@ ₹35 Lakhs each), high-power concert PA sound systems, GaN fast chargers & vehicle AC inverters, 4K laser projectors, 86" interactive displays, cinema cameras, and electronic/robotics testing labs for Krishi Kshetra in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification covers all physical asset acquisitions required under our **₹50.00 Crore** seed funding round:
* **Land Plot Acquisition & Permanent Civil Campus Construction (25,000 sq. ft.)**: ₹10.00 Crores.
* **Temporary First Office Renovation, Electrical Rewiring & Furniture**: ₹2.00 Crores.
* **500kVA Industrial HT Transformer Substation**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition Fleet (AX7 L AWD - 6 Units @ ₹35 Lakhs each)**: ₹2.10 Crores.
* **Concert-Grade PA Sound Systems, Subwoofers & Digital Mixers**: ₹0.60 Crores.
* **GaN Fast Chargers, Power Banks & Vehicle 230V Inverters**: ₹0.30 Crores.
* **High-Lumen 4K Laser Projectors & 86" Interactive Displays**: ₹1.00 Crore.
* **Custom AIO Liquid-Cooled Desktop Builds (RTX 5090/5080 Rigs)**: ₹4.50 Crores.
* **High-Performance Gaming Laptops Fleet (ASUS ROG / Razer / MSI)**: ₹3.00 Crores.
* **Unlisted Corporate Studio & Cinema Camera Fleet (Sony FX6/FX3, RED Komodo, Drones)**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **Robotics, Electronic & Optical Lab Testing Equipment**: ₹1.50 Crores.
* **SMT Assembly Line, 5-Axis CNC Mill & 250kW Solar Microgrid**: ₹2.00 Crores.

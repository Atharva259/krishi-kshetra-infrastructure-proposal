## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's headquarters, R&D labs, cloud platform operations, and engineering teams. It establishes financial controls, operating guidelines, software subscription costs, facilities leases, and salary structures for the initial 12-month seed cycle.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation (salaries and stipends).
* Facilities rent, utilities, and high-speed communications.
* Cloud computing infrastructure hosting (AWS/Azure) and third-party SaaS tools.
* Legal retainer fees, insurance, audit compliance, and general administration.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

## 1. Executive Summary
The Year 1 Operating Expenditure (OPEX) budget for Krishi Kshetra is established at **$70,000**, representing **36.8%** of the total **$190,000** seed funding round. 

By utilizing our custom 15kW solar microgrid to offset utility costs and prioritizing lean, core engineering compensation during the prototyping phase, the monthly operational burn rate is controlled at an average of **$5,833/month**. This allocation ensures that the company remains fully operational for 12 months, achieving all major technical validation milestones.

---

## 2. OPEX Objectives
1. **Maintain Financial Runways**: Ensure a guaranteed 12-month operational runway to execute product prototyping and local pilot programs without running out of capital.
2. **Lean Personnel Spending**: Compensate a highly focused team of mechatronics, embedded firmware, and backend software engineers to build the core platform.
3. **Optimized Cloud Deployment**: Leverage cloud-native free tier architectures and incubator hosting credits (AWS Activate) to minimize computing expenses.
4. **Compliance & Audit Preparedness**: Support mandatory legal, insurance, and audit structures to maintain commercial transparency for future Series A rounds.

## 7. Legal, Compliance, & Audit Overhead
To protect our intellectual property (IP) and satisfy government incubation requirements:
* **Legal Retainer**: $200/month ($2,400/annually) for IP patent filings, employment contract drafting, and vendor lease agreements.
* **Audit & Accounting Fees**: $150/month ($1,800/annually) for monthly book-keeping, payroll tax filings, and quarterly financial reviews.
* **Insurances**: $150/month ($1,800/annually) for general liability, laboratory machinery insurance, and worker compensation packages.

## 8. OPEX Risk Management & Buffer Allocations
Operating deep tech startups entails unexpected expenditures. To protect operations from unforeseen cash outflows:
* A cash reserve buffer of **$300/month** ($3,600/annually) is set aside.
* If unpaid, this buffer accumulates as an interest-bearing cash reserve, extending our runway beyond month 12.
* **Opex Mitigation Actions**: If the funding round is delayed, stipends are temporarily suspended, and non-essential cloud servers are scaled down to minimum configurations.

## 9. Operational Efficiency Targets
* **Solar Offset Yield**: Achieve a minimum of **$250/month in utility savings** through active solar load balancing.
* **SaaS Consolidation**: Audit team subscriptions quarterly to prune unused software seats.
* **AWS Cost Control**: Set up automated CloudWatch billing alarms that trigger Slack notifications if server runtime costs exceed $10/day.

## 10. Glossary
* **OPEX**: Operating Expenditures; the ongoing costs for running a product, business, or system.
* **ANR**: Application Not Responding; an Android OS dialog shown when the UI thread is blocked for too long.
* **SaaS**: Software as a Service; a software licensing and delivery model in which software is licensed on a subscription basis.
* **AWS Activate**: A startup program by Amazon providing free hosting credits and support resources.
* **Runway**: The amount of time a company has before it runs out of cash, calculated as current cash divided by monthly burn rate.

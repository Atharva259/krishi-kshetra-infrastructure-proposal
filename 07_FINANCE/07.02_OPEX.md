## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, multi-SIM cellular failover network, Mahindra XUV700 AWD fleet diesel & maintenance, enterprise AI tools, Unity 3D engines, outdoor advertising campaigns, video production field trips, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**.
* **High-Speed Fiber, Satellite & Multi-SIM 5G Cellular Network (₹1.50 Crores/Year)**: Dual 10 Gbps fiber + Starlink satellite + **Multi-operator 5G/4G Enterprise SIM data plans (Airtel, Jio, Vi)**.
* **Mahindra XUV700 AWD Fleet Diesel Fuel, Servicing & Insurance (₹0.50 Crores/Year)**: Diesel fuel, routine servicing, off-road tire replacements, and insurance for the 4 Mahindra XUV700 AWD field vehicles.
* **Outdoor Advertising, Concert PA Systems, Expo Booths & Video Trips (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.

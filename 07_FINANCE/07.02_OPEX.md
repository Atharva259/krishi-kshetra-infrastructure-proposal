## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores). It establishes financial controls, operating guidelines, software subscriptions, facility leases, and salary structures for our **₹32 Crore** seed round.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, utilities, and leased lines.
* Cloud computing infrastructure hosting (AWS/Azure) and enterprise software licenses.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

## 1. Executive Summary
The Year 1 Operating Expenditure (OPEX) budget for Krishi Kshetra is established at **₹12.00 Crores (₹1,200 Lakhs)**, representing **37.5%** of the total **₹32.00 Crores (₹3,200 Lakhs)** seed funding round. 

By utilizing our custom 250kW solar microgrid to offset campus utility costs and maintaining high capital discipline, the monthly operational burn rate is controlled at an average of **₹1.00 Crore/month (₹100 Lakhs/month)**. This allocation ensures a guaranteed 18-month operational runway to deploy 10,000 nodes and 100 rovers across 50,000 acres.

---

## 2. OPEX Objectives
1. **Maintain Financial Runway**: Ensure a guaranteed 18-month operational runway to execute hardware manufacturing, software refinement, and pilot programs without running out of capital.
2. **Top-Tier Engineering Team**: Compensate a high-caliber team of 25+ senior engineers across Mechatronics, Embedded Firmware, AI Computer Vision, Kotlin Mobile, Cloud DevOps, and Agronomy.
3. **Regional Hub Presence**: Lease and staff 3 regional field support hubs in key agricultural clusters to handle field deployment, rover maintenance, and farmer onboarding.
4. **IP Protection & Certification**: Support global (USPTO) and domestic (IPO) patent filings and complete WPC/BIS/CE certifications for commercial hardware launch.

## 8. OPEX Risk Management & Buffer Allocations
* An operational contingency buffer of **₹8,33,334/month (₹1.00 Crore/annually)** is established to absorb component price swings or extended pilot timelines.
* **Opex Burn Cap**: If monthly operational burn exceeds **₹1.10 Crores/month**, mandatory freezing of non-essential recruitment is triggered automatically.

## 9. Operational Efficiency Targets
* **Solar Utility Savings**: Achieve a minimum of **₹3,50,000/month in utility savings** via the 250kW solar array.
* **Hardware Manufacturing Yield**: Maintain SMT assembly pass rate above **99.2%** to eliminate component rework waste.

## 10. Glossary
* **OPEX**: Operating Expenditures; ongoing costs required to run a business.
* **USPTO**: United States Patent and Trademark Office; global intellectual property authority.
* **WPC**: Wireless Planning & Coordination Wing; Indian authority regulating radio frequency spectrum.
* **BIS**: Bureau of Indian Standards; national standards body certifying hardware product safety.
* **Runway**: The period of time a company can operate before exhausting available cash reserves.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, enterprise AI tools, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **High-Speed Internet & Satellite Connectivity (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**: Dual symmetrical 10 Gbps enterprise fiber leased lines (Airtel/Tata/Jio Enterprise with 99.99% SLA) + Starlink/OneWeb LEO satellite backup terminals + BGP auto-failover & static IP blocks.
* **Enterprise AI & Software SaaS Tools (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**: Anthropic Claude Enterprise, OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise/API, Moonshot Kimi AI, Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, and JetBrains IDEs.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

## 3. High-Speed Internet & Satellite Connectivity (₹2.00 Crores)

To support continuous telemetry data streaming from thousands of Bhumika-1 nodes, rapid AI model weight syncing, and zero-latency video feeds, **₹2.00 Crores/year** is dedicated exclusively to connectivity:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           HIGH-SPEED CONNECTIVITY ARCHITECTURE (₹2.00 CR/YR)            │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Primary & Secondary Fiber    │ Satellite & Wireless Backup              │
│ • Dual 10 Gbps Symmetrical   │ • Starlink / OneWeb Enterprise Terminal  │
│   Enterprise Leased Lines    │ • BGP Auto-Failover & Static IP Subnet   │
│ • 99.99% Availability SLA    │ • Wi-Fi 7 Campus Mesh & 100G Fiber LAN   │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Itemized Breakdown (₹2.00 Crores / Year):
* **Dual Redundant 10 Gbps Symmetrical Enterprise Fiber Lines**: ₹12,50,000/month (₹1.50 Crores/annually) for dual 10 Gbps leased lines from Tier-1 ISPs (Airtel Business & Tata Communications) with 99.99% uptime SLA guarantees and BGP auto-failover routing.
* **Starlink / OneWeb LEO Satellite Backup Dish**: ₹2,50,000/month (₹0.30 Crores/annually) for Low-Earth Orbit satellite dish terminals for zero-outage redundancy during extreme weather or terrestrial cable cuts.
* **Static IPv4/v6 Subnets & Peering**: ₹1,66,667/month (₹0.20 Crores/annually) for dedicated static IP blocks and direct cloud peering.

## 5. Glossary
* **10 Gbps Symmetrical**: Dedicated high-speed internet connection offering 10 Gigabits per second upload and download speed.
* **BGP Failover**: Automated routing switching internet traffic to secondary provider upon primary line failure.
* **Claude Enterprise**: Anthropic's flagship enterprise AI platform.
* **ChatGPT Enterprise**: OpenAI's enterprise AI platform featuring GPT-4o models.
* **Kimi AI**: High-context-window AI engine for long-document analysis.

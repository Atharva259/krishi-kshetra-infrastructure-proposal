## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, multi-SIM cellular failover network, Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition (AX7 L AWD) fleet diesel & maintenance, enterprise AI tools, Unity 3D engines, outdoor advertising campaigns, video production field trips, unlisted studio recording operations, long-range 8K drone filming, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows under our **₹20.00 Crore** Year 1 OPEX pool:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 35+ senior engineers, videographers, and leadership (**₹8.50 Crores/year**).
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹3.50 Crores/Year)**: Unity Industry/Enterprise, Unreal Engine 5, Autodesk Collection (Fusion 360/Maya/3ds Max), Houdini, Anthropic Claude Enterprise, OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise/API, Moonshot Kimi AI, Adobe Creative Cloud.
* **High-Speed Fiber, Satellite & Multi-SIM 5G Cellular Network (₹2.50 Crores/Year)**: Dual 10 Gbps symmetrical fiber leased lines + Starlink LEO satellite + multi-operator Airtel/Jio/Vi enterprise 5G SIM failover.
* **Outdoor Advertising, Unlisted Studio Production, 8K Drone Filming & Video Trips (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**: Rural highway billboards, APMC mandi ads, Kisan Expo pavilions, unlisted studio content editing, YouTube product reviews, and 8K aerial drone filming of large-area farm trials.
* **Campus & 5 Regional Field Hub Leases (₹1.50 Crores/Year)**.
* **Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition Fleet Diesel Fuel, Servicing & Insurance (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**: Diesel fuel, routine servicing, off-road tire replacements, and insurance for 6 Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition SUVs.
* **Global Patent Filings, Legal Retainers & Reserve Buffer (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.

## 2. Outdoor Advertising, Unlisted Studio & 8K Drone Filming (₹2.00 Crores)

To fund video recording, YouTube reviews, APMC mandi billboards, and 8K aerial farm filming:
* **Outdoor APMC Mandi Billboards & Highway Ads (₹80 Lakhs/year)**: High-visibility hoardings across agricultural market yards and national highways.
* **Unlisted Studio Production & Video Editing Logistics (₹60 Lakhs/year)**: Video editing, color grading, multi-camera audio mixing, and production studio ops.
* **8K Drone Large-Area Filming & Field Trip Logistics (₹60 Lakhs/year)**: Remote farm travel, dual-operator drone filming logistics, battery charging generators, and field trip production.

## 3. Glossary
* **Unlisted Studio Production**: Corporate video production facility creating unlisted YouTube review videos, investor walkthroughs, and farmer training materials.
* **8K Drone Filming**: Aerial cinematography covering far-distance and large-area farm trials using the DJI Inspire 3 8K system.

## 1. Executive Summary
The Year 1 Operating Expenditure (OPEX) budget for Krishi Kshetra is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **40.0%** of the total **₹50.00 Crores (₹5,000 Lakhs)** seed funding round.

## 2. Manufacturing Raw Materials & Testing Calibration Costs (₹1.20 Crores)

To sustain continuous hardware manufacturing, prototyping, and lab testing throughout Year 1:
* **SMT Electronic Components & Raw PCB Stock (₹60 Lakhs/year)**: Microcontrollers, wireless transceivers, power ICs, sensors, SMD passives, and multi-layer bare PCBs for telemetry board production.
* **CNC Aluminum Ingots & Carbon Fiber Materials (₹40 Lakhs/year)**: 6061 aircraft-grade aluminum billets, continuous carbon fiber spools, stainless steel fasteners, and CNC coolant.
* **Lab Testing Calibration & Certification (₹20 Lakhs/year)**: ISO 17025 equipment calibration, RF compliance testing, soil chemistry reagents, and environmental test chamber consumables.

---

## 3. Glossary
* **SMT Passives**: Surface-mount resistors, capacitors, and inductors used on printed circuit boards.
* **ISO 17025 Calibration**: Accredited calibration ensuring measurement accuracy for lab testing gear.

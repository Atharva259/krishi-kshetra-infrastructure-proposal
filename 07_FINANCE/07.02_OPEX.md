## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, enterprise AI tools, Unity 3D engines, outdoor advertising campaigns, video production field trips, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**.
* **High-Speed Internet & Satellite Connectivity (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.
* **Outdoor Advertising, PR, Expo Booths & Field Trip Video Production (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**: Billboard campaigns across rural agri-zones, Kisan Expo pavilion booths, PR outreach, and video recording logistics for outdoor field trips & talks.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.

## 3. Outdoor Advertising, PR & Video Production (₹1.00 Crore)

To build strong national brand equity and document field deployments:
* **Outdoor Advertising & Billboard Campaigns (₹40,00,000/year)**: High-visibility outdoor billboards along major rural highway corridors and agricultural APMC mandi hubs across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Telangana.
* **Trade Expo Booths & Talks (₹30,00,000/year)**: Dedicated exhibition pavilions at major agricultural expos (Kisan Agri Show, AgTech Summit, ICAR Conferences) and keynote talk sponsorships.
* **Field Trip Video Production & YouTube Logistics (₹30,00,000/year)**: Travel, crew, and editing logistics for filming outdoor field deployments, farmer interviews, YouTube technical series, and investor updates.

---

## 4. Glossary
* **APMC Mandi**: Agricultural Produce Market Committee trading hubs where regional farmers gather.
* **Field Trip Production**: On-location video recording of rover deployments and agronomic trials.

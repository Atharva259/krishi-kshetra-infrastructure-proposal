## 📄 Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to maintain Krishi Kshetra's R&D campus, high-speed mobile fleets, and engineering teams over an extended 3-year runway under the **₹120.00 Crore Master Envelope**.

The total OPEX allocation is **₹15.00 Crores** (plus an internal ₹0.02 Crore allocation for Mobile Data/SIM OPEX pulled from the master pool).

---

## 🏗️ Extended 3-Year OPEX Breakdown (₹15.00 Crores)

| Operational Category | Allocation (₹ Crores) | Strategic Focus & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Extended 3-Year Baseline OPEX** | **₹14.98 Crores** | 36 months of core facility management, developer compensation buffers, continuous utility overheads, and dark-fiber connectivity retainers. |
| **Mobile SIM Cards & Cellular Data Plans** | **₹0.02 Crores** | Enterprise corporate bulk plans (Airtel Business/JioEnterprise) providing high-cap 5G data bundles across all 9 mobile devices (₹1,500-₹2,000/device/month). |

**TOTAL OPEX: ₹15.00 Crores**

---

## 🔒 Extended Operational Strategy

The extended operational runway is designed to protect the core business from cash-flow interruptions during early product cycles and deep-tech hardware iterations. By securing a 36-month buffer, the engineering teams are insulated from external fundraising pressures, allowing them to focus entirely on autonomous robotics and AI model training.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, enterprise AI tools, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Connectivity & Enterprise AI/SaaS Subscriptions (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**: High-speed dual 10 Gbps fiber lines, Starlink satellite backup, Anthropic Claude Enterprise, OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise/API, Kimi AI long-context engine, Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, and JetBrains IDEs.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

By utilizing our custom 250kW solar microgrid to offset campus utility costs and maintaining high capital discipline, the monthly operational burn rate is controlled at an average of **₹1.00 Crore/month (₹100 Lakhs/month)**.

## 3. Connectivity & Enterprise AI/SaaS Subscriptions Suite

To equip our software engineers, AI researchers, and branding teams with state-of-the-art foundation models, creative software, and zero-outage connectivity, **₹2.00 Crores/year** is allocated:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           INTERNET & ENTERPRISE AI/SAAS SUITE (₹2.00 CR/YR)             │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ High-Speed Internet (₹1.00Cr)│ Enterprise AI & Creative Tools (₹1.00Cr)│
│ • Dual 10 Gbps Enterprise    │ • Anthropic Claude Enterprise & API     │
│   Fiber Leased Lines         │ • OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise & GPT-4o API │
│ • Starlink / OneWeb LEO      │ • Kimi AI Long-Context Engine           │
│   Satellite Backup Dish      │ • Adobe Creative Cloud Enterprise Suite  │
│ • BGP Auto-Failover & IP Net │ • Figma, JetBrains & GitHub Enterprise   │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Itemized Breakdown (₹2.00 Crores / Year):
* **High-Speed Internet Connectivity (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**:
  * Dual redundant symmetrical 10 Gbps enterprise fiber leased lines (Airtel Business & Tata Communications) with 99.99% uptime SLA guarantees and BGP auto-failover routing.
  * Starlink / OneWeb enterprise LEO satellite backup dish terminal for zero-outage redundancy.
  * Static IPv4/v6 subnets and direct cloud peering.
* **Enterprise AI Foundation Models & SaaS Tools (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**:
  * **Anthropic Claude Enterprise & API**: Enterprise access for Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Opus for automated code refactoring, complex reasoning, and technical documentation synthesis.
  * **OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise & API**: ChatGPT Team/Enterprise seats + API tokens for GPT-4o multimodal vision analysis.
  * **Kimi AI Long-Context Engine**: Enterprise API tokens for Moonshot Kimi AI for processing multi-hundred-page agricultural research papers and agronomic datasets.
  * **Adobe Creative Cloud Enterprise**: Complete suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects) for hardware UI design, product branding, and investor pitch assets.
  * **Engineering Tools**: JetBrains All Products Pack, Figma Enterprise UI, and GitHub Enterprise.

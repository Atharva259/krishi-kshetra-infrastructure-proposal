## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, enterprise AI tools, Unity/Unreal real-time 3D simulation engines, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**: Unity Industry/Enterprise, Unreal Engine 5 simulation pipelines, Autodesk Product Design & Manufacturing Collection (Fusion 360 / Maya / 3ds Max), SideFX Houdini, Substance 3D, Anthropic Claude Enterprise, OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise/API, Kimi AI, Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, and JetBrains IDEs.
* **High-Speed Internet & Satellite Connectivity (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**: Dual symmetrical 10 Gbps enterprise fiber leased lines + Starlink/OneWeb LEO satellite backup terminals.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

### 3.1 Itemized Breakdown (₹2.00 Crores / Year):
* **Unity Industry & Unreal Engine 5 Real-Time 3D Suite (₹40,00,000/year)**:
  * Unity Industry/Enterprise seats for building real-time digital twin farm environments, ROS 2 rover kinematics simulations, and interactive agronomy training modules.
  * Unreal Engine 5 custom simulation plugins, SpeedTree foliage procedural tools, and high-fidelity photogrammetry assets.
* **Autodesk 3D CAD & Animation Suite (₹40,00,000/year)**:
  * Autodesk Product Design & Manufacturing Collection (Fusion 360, Inventor, Maya, 3ds Max) for generative mechanical chassis design, structural FEA stress testing, and photorealistic 3D rendering.
  * SideFX Houdini for procedural terrain modeling, soil erosion physics simulations, and particle fluid dynamics (pesticide spray coverage).
* **Enterprise AI Foundation Models (₹80,00,000/year)**:
  * Anthropic Claude Enterprise & API (Claude 3.5 Sonnet/Opus for automated code refactoring and technical synthesis).
  * OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise & API (GPT-4o multimodal vision analysis).
  * Moonshot Kimi AI (Long-context analysis of agronomic research papers).
* **Adobe Creative Cloud, Substance 3D & Developer SaaS (₹40,00,000/year)**:
  * Adobe Creative Cloud Enterprise + Adobe Substance 3D Painter/Sampler for PBR texture authoring, UI/UX design, and branding.
  * JetBrains IDEs, Figma Enterprise UI, and GitHub Enterprise CI/CD runners.

## 4. High-Speed Internet & Satellite Connectivity (₹1.00 Crore)

Provides dedicated high-speed internet and zero-outage satellite connectivity (**₹1.00 Crore/year**):
* **Dual 10 Gbps Enterprise Fiber Lines**: Symmetrical 10 Gbps leased lines from Tier-1 ISPs with 99.99% SLA guarantees.
* **Starlink / OneWeb Satellite Backup**: LEO satellite dish terminals for zero-outage redundancy.
* **Static IP Blocks & BGP Routing**: Dedicated IPv4/v6 subnets and cloud peering.

## 5. Glossary
* **Unity Industry**: Enterprise edition of Unity engine tailored for digital twins, robotics simulation, and CAD visualization.
* **Unreal Engine 5**: High-fidelity real-time 3D game engine used for photorealistic environmental and sensor simulation.
* **Houdini**: Procedural 3D software for physical simulation of fluids, dynamics, and terrain geometry.
* **Claude Enterprise**: Anthropic's flagship enterprise AI platform.
* **ChatGPT Enterprise**: OpenAI's enterprise AI platform featuring GPT-4o models.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Dedicated High-Speed Internet Connectivity**: Dual redundant 10 Gbps enterprise fiber leased lines (Airtel/Tata/Jio Enterprise with 99.99% SLA) + Starlink/OneWeb satellite backup terminals.
* Cloud computing infrastructure hosting (AWS/Azure) and enterprise software licenses.
* Legal retainer fees, global & Indian patent filings (IPO/USPTO), WPC/BIS certifications, audit compliance, and operational reserves.
It excludes initial physical asset acquisition costs, which are covered in [07.01 CAPEX](./07.01_CAPEX.md).

## 3. High-Speed Internet & Connectivity Infrastructure

To support continuous telemetry data streaming from thousands of Bhumika-1 nodes, rapid AI model weight syncing, and zero-latency video feeds:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 HIGH-SPEED CONNECTIVITY ARCHITECTURE                    │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Primary & Secondary Fiber    │ Satellite & Wireless Backup              │
│ • Dual 10 Gbps Symmetrical   │ • Starlink / OneWeb Enterprise Terminal  │
│   Enterprise Leased Lines    │ • BGP Auto-Failover & Static IP Subnet   │
│ • 99.99% Availability SLA    │ • Wi-Fi 7 Campus Mesh & 100G Fiber LAN   │
└──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Itemized High-Speed Connectivity Suite (₹1.50 Crores / Year):
* **Dual Redundant 10 Gbps Enterprise Fiber Lines**: ₹8,33,333/month (₹1.00 Crore/annually) for dual symmetrical 10 Gbps leased lines from Tier-1 ISPs (Airtel Business & Tata Communications) with 99.99% uptime SLA guarantees and BGP auto-failover routing.
* **Enterprise Satellite Internet Backup**: ₹2,50,000/month (₹0.30 Crores/annually) for Starlink / OneWeb Low-Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite dish terminals, ensuring continuous connectivity during severe weather or local terrestrial cable cuts.
* **Static IPv4/IPv6 Subnet & CDN Sync**: ₹1,66,667/month (₹0.20 Crores/annually) for dedicated static IP blocks, direct AWS DirectConnect peering, and high-frequency edge sync.

## 4. Glossary
* **10 Gbps Symmetrical**: High-speed internet connection offering 10 Gigabits per second upload and download speed.
* **BGP Failover**: Border Gateway Protocol automated routing that instantly switches internet traffic to the secondary provider if the primary line fails.
* **SLA**: Service Level Agreement; formal guarantee of network uptime and latency.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, multi-SIM cellular failover network, Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition (AX7 L AWD) fleet diesel & maintenance, manufacturing raw materials (6061-T6 aluminum billets, carbon fiber filament, 3D resins, SMT components), lab calibration services, enterprise AI tools, Unity 3D engines, outdoor advertising campaigns, video production field trips, media production operations, long-range 8K drone filming, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹50 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows under our **₹20.00 Crore** Year 1 OPEX pool:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 35+ senior engineers, videographers, and leadership (**₹8.50 Crores/year**).
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹3.50 Crores/Year)**: Unity Industry/Enterprise, Unreal Engine 5, Autodesk Collection (Fusion 360/Maya/3ds Max), Houdini, Anthropic Claude Enterprise, OpenAI ChatGPT Enterprise/API, Moonshot Kimi AI, Adobe Creative Cloud.
* **High-Speed Fiber, Satellite & Multi-SIM 5G Cellular Network (₹2.50 Crores/Year)**: Dual 10 Gbps symmetrical fiber leased lines + Starlink LEO satellite + multi-operator Airtel/Jio/Vi enterprise 5G SIM failover.
* **Outdoor Advertising, Media Production, 8K Drone Filming & Video Trips (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**.
* **Manufacturing Raw Materials (Metal Chassis & 3D Printed Bodies) & Testing Calibration Operations (₹1.20 Crores/Year)**: 6061-T6 aluminum billets, structural steel tubing, Markforged micro-carbon fiber Onyx filament, UV-stabilized ASA 3D resins, raw SMT electronic components, bare PCBs, ISO lab calibration, and testing certification.
* **Campus & 5 Regional Field Hub Leases (₹1.30 Crores/Year)**.
* **Mahindra XUV 7XO Ebony Edition Fleet Diesel Fuel, Servicing & Insurance (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.
* **Global Patent Filings, Legal Retainers & Reserve Buffer (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.

## 1. Executive Summary
The Year 1 Operating Expenditure (OPEX) budget for Krishi Kshetra is established at **₹20.00 Crores (₹2,000 Lakhs)**, representing **40.0%** of the total **₹50.00 Crores (₹5,000 Lakhs)** seed funding round.

---

## 2. Manufacturing Raw Materials & Testing Calibration Costs (₹1.20 Crores)

To sustain continuous hardware manufacturing, chassis fabrication, 3D body printing, prototyping, and lab testing throughout Year 1:
* **SMT Electronic Components & Raw PCB Stock (₹50 Lakhs/year)**: Microcontrollers, wireless transceivers, power ICs, sensors, SMD passives, and multi-layer bare PCBs.
* **Metal Chassis Billets & 3D Printing Body Materials (₹50 Lakhs/year)**: 6061-T6 aircraft aluminum billets, structural steel tubing, Markforged micro-carbon fiber Onyx filament, UV-stabilized ASA 3D resins, stainless steel hardware, and anodizing chemicals.
* **Lab Testing Calibration & Certification (₹20 Lakhs/year)**: ISO 17025 equipment calibration, RF compliance testing, soil chemistry reagents, and environmental test chamber consumables.

## 3. Glossary
* **6061-T6 Aluminum**: High-strength, corrosion-resistant aircraft-grade aluminum alloy used for CNC robot frames.
* **Markforged Onyx Filament**: Micro-carbon fiber reinforced nylon 3D printing material for ultra-tough drone bodies.

## 📄 Purpose & Scope

### Purpose
This document specifies the recurring Operating Expenditures (OPEX) required to run Krishi Kshetra's tech campus, R&D labs, regional field hubs, cloud computing operations, high-speed internet infrastructure, multi-SIM cellular failover network, 4x4 fleet diesel & maintenance, enterprise AI tools, Unity 3D engines, outdoor advertising campaigns, video production field trips, and engineering teams in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹ Crores) under our **₹32 Crore** seed funding proposal.

### Scope
This specification includes all recurring operational outflows, such as:
* Personnel compensation for a team of 25+ senior engineers and leadership.
* Tech campus rent (15,000 sq. ft.), 3 regional field hub leases, and facilities maintenance.
* **Enterprise AI, Unity Real-Time 3D & Game Engine Software Suite (₹2.00 Crores/Year)**.
* **High-Speed Fiber, Satellite & Multi-SIM 5G Cellular Network (₹1.50 Crores/Year)**: Dual 10 Gbps fiber + Starlink satellite + **Multi-operator 5G/4G Enterprise SIM data plans (Airtel, Jio, Vi)** for zero-outage automatic failover when fiber lines fail.

* **Outdoor Advertising, PR, Expo Booths & Field Trip Video Production (₹1.00 Crore/Year)**.

## 3. Fleet Diesel Fuel & Off-Road Maintenance (₹0.50 Crores)

To operate our 4x4 all-terrain vehicle fleet across remote agricultural regions:
* **Diesel Fuel Consumption**: ₹25,00,000/year for high-mileage rural field trips and rover transport.
* **Fleet Maintenance & Servicing**: ₹15,00,000/year for scheduled oil changes, heavy-duty off-road tire replacements, winch servicing, and mechanical repairs.
* **Comprehensive Fleet Insurance**: ₹10,00,000/year for commercial all-terrain vehicle insurance coverage.

---

## 4. Multi-Operator 5G/4G SIM Cellular Failover Network (₹0.50 Crores)

To ensure zero connectivity downtime when physical fiber cables fail:
* **Multi-Carrier Enterprise SIM Subscriptions**: ₹35,00,000/year for unlimited high-speed 5G/4G enterprise SIM data plans across all major operators (Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vi) installed in dual/quad-SIM industrial cellular routers on rovers, mobile test rigs, and campus gateways.
* **Automatic Failover Gateway Routing**: ₹15,00,000/year for cloud-managed SD-WAN multi-carrier bonding software.

---

## 5. Glossary
* **Multi-SIM Failover**: Network architecture that automatically switches data traffic to mobile cellular networks (Airtel/Jio/Vi) if terrestrial fiber lines drop out.
* **4x4 Fleet Maintenance**: Off-road vehicle maintenance including suspension tuning, tire replacement, and engine servicing.

## 🚀 Sovereign Software Architecture

Krishi Kshetra's AI models are developed, trained, and validated entirely on proprietary, internally-managed hardware, completely decoupled from third-party cloud compute providers.

## 🏗️ Model Training Pipeline

### 1. Data Ingestion & Storage
Raw agricultural data (spectral imaging, IoT telemetry, drone footage) is ingested via dual-ISP BGP edge routers and stored securely on the multi-petabyte corporate NAS arrays.

### 2. Local Training Execution
Engineers initiate training runs from their RTX 5090-equipped custom desktop workstations. Heavy compilation and multi-epoch deep learning tasks are distributed to the single-tank immersion compute nodes. Because all hardware is physically connected via dark fiber/100GbE internally, dataset transfer latency is near zero.

### 3. Edge Deployment
Compiled models are pushed directly to the autonomous robotic units built in our CNC/SMT manufacturing labs. The models are protected via Quantum-Resistant Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) prior to firmware flashing.

## 🛡️ Security & Privacy
Since data never leaves the Krishi Kshetra physical campus (secured by biometric vaults, 4K optical surveillance, and HSM encryption), we guarantee absolute intellectual property sovereignty to our investors.
